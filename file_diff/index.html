Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “File Diff”

App name
File Diff
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “File Diff” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App compares two pieces of text, two files, two folders, or two images and shows what changed. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. What You Compare Stays on Your Device

This is the most important point in this Policy, so it comes first.

  • Every comparison is performed entirely on your device. The text, files, folders, and images you compare are never uploaded, transmitted, or copied to our servers or to any third party.
  • We never see what you compare. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server component to the App.
  • The App contains no code that sends your content anywhere. The only network access it makes is for serving advertisements, as described in Section 3.

Files and folders are read only when you choose them yourself through your device’s own file picker, and only for as long as the comparison needs them.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration or login.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect the following:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

This advertising data is handled by Google, not by us, and is unrelated to the content you compare.

3. Purpose of Use

  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects

4. Permissions

The App requests only the permissions it genuinely needs:

  • Internet access and network state — used solely to load advertisements.

The App does not request storage, microphone, camera, location, or contacts permissions. Reading the files and folders you compare requires no permission, because your device’s file picker passes along only the items you select.

5. Data Stored on Your Device

Comparison history

The App keeps a short history of your recent comparisons so you can find them again. The history does not store the content you compared. Each entry records only:

  • The date and time of the comparison
  • The comparison mode (text, file, folder, or image)
  • The display names of the two items, and for file, folder, and image comparisons, their locations on your device so they can be opened again
  • The number of differences found, and whether you marked the entry as a favourite

Because the content itself is not kept, a text comparison cannot be restored from the history. The history holds a limited number of the most recent entries; older entries are discarded automatically. You can delete individual entries, or clear the history entirely, from the history screen at any time.

Settings

Your app settings, such as theme, language, and display preferences, are stored only on your device.

All of this data is stored locally using your device’s standard app storage. It is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

6. Receiving Content Shared From Other Apps (Android)

On Android, you can send text or a text file to the App using your device’s Share function. The shared content is passed straight into the comparison screen in memory. It is not saved to a file, retained after the comparison, or transmitted anywhere. The App does not declare itself as a destination for photos or videos. This feature is not currently available on iOS.

7. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

8. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide user information to third parties, except where disclosure is required by law. For how advertising data is handled, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

9. Consent for Personalized Advertising

For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, the App requests consent for personalized advertising through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can change your consent choices at any time from the App’s settings screen. On iOS, the App requests tracking permission via the AppTrackingTransparency framework; all features remain available if you decline.

10. Your Rights

Because the App has no account and stores your comparison history and settings only on your device, we hold no personal data about you that we could retrieve, correct, or delete on your behalf. You are in direct control of everything the App stores: clear the history from the history screen, or uninstall the App to remove all of it.

For advertising data collected by Google, you can withdraw or change your consent at any time from the App’s settings screen, and you may exercise your rights regarding that data directly with Google using the links in Section 8.

11. Opting Out of the Advertising ID

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Reset advertising ID” (menu names may vary by device)
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

12. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

13. Disclaimer Regarding Comparison Results

The App shows the differences between two items as a convenience. It is not a certified verification tool, and its results should not be treated on their own as proof that two documents, files, or images are identical or that they differ. For very large or almost entirely different inputs, the App groups changes more coarsely so that it can respond promptly, and the result, while correct, may not be the smallest possible set of differences. Image comparison treats very small colour variations as identical so that a re-saved photograph does not appear to differ everywhere. Where the outcome carries legal, financial, or safety consequences, please verify it by other means as well. The developer accepts no liability for any loss arising from actions taken on the basis of the App’s results.

14. Security

We take appropriate measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or leakage of the information handled by the App. Because what you compare never leaves your device, it is protected by your device’s own security, such as your screen lock and storage encryption.

15. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be revised without prior notice due to changes in laws or in the App’s features. The revised policy takes effect once posted on this page.
