Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Personality Test”

App name
Personality Test
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Personality Test” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App is a personality quiz for fun: you answer everyday questions and play short mini games. It shows one of 20 personality types, your five trait scores drawn as a constellation, and a surprising side of you where your answers split apart.

1. Summary

  • Your answers and results are never sent to us. Your result is worked out entirely on your device. We have no server that receives your answers or results.
  • There is no account registration and no login. The App never asks for or collects your name, age, date of birth, gender, email address, phone number, or address.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your answers or results.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

Advertising data is never combined with your answers, results, or anything else the App stores on your device.

3. Your Answers and Results

  • Worked out on your device only. The answers you choose, your mini game results, and the trait scores, the type and the surprising side worked out from them are all computed and stored on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party, unless you choose to share a result (section 7).
  • No personal details are needed. The App never asks for your name, age, date of birth, or gender; your result is worked out only from how you play.
  • No camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location. The App does not ask for access to any of these, and does not use location services.

4. Network Use

The quiz works entirely offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), as described in sections 2 and 10. Before loading an ad, the App may look up the ad server’s name to check that it is online; no information about you is sent in that check.
  • Links you tap. Opening a store page or this policy hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

The advertising service starts when you open the App, so that an ad can be loaded in advance and shown once just before each result is revealed, the first one included. Banner ads appear at the bottom of the settings and FAQ screens.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To run the quiz and show your result
  • To keep your record of results, so you can see your past results and which types you have met
  • To avoid repeating questions you have already seen
  • To remember your settings, such as your display language
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects, using what you tell us and the aggregated statistics provided by the app stores

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored on your device, is never transmitted by the App to our servers or to any third party unless you choose to share a result (section 7), and is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App:

  • Your record of results: for each quiz, the date and time, the answers you chose, your mini game results, your five trait scores, the type it revealed, and the surprising side
  • The numbers of the questions already shown (the latest 120), so the next quiz can avoid them
  • Your chosen display language
  • For the review prompt: how many quizzes you have finished, when you chose “later”, and whether it has been shown

Your advertising consent choices are also stored on your device. Google’s advertising services read them to decide how ads may be served, and they may be sent with ad requests.

Backups. On Android, the App turns off device backup and transfer, so this data is not copied to your Google account or to a new device. On iOS, it may be included in the backups you make of your device with iCloud or a computer. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, and we cannot access them.

You can delete your results one at a time, or all at once, from the App’s record screen. There is no account and no sign-in, so your records are not linked to your identity.

7. Sharing Your Result

When you tap a share button, the App draws an image of your result on your device and hands it, with a short text, to the X app or to the operating system’s share sheet. The image is written to the App’s temporary storage only for sharing and is never sent to us. Where it goes is your choice, and the app you share to applies its own privacy policy. Sharing the App from the settings screen hands only a short text and the App’s store link to the share sheet.

8. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). When to ask is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

9. Entertainment Purpose

The App is for entertainment only. Your result comes from how you answer and play in that round. It is inspired by published research, but it is not a medical or psychological test, it is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose anything. No type is better or worse than another, and a result says nothing about your health, your character, or your abilities. Please do not use it to label other people. If you are worried about your mental health, please talk to a doctor or a qualified professional. Short research facts shown in the App are for interest only.

10. Consent for Personalized Advertising

For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, and where otherwise required by law, the App requests consent for personalized advertising through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can review and change your consent choices at any time from the App’s settings screen, where this option appears if consent applies to you.

On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalized advertising only if you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow it, the App still works in full and ads are shown without personalization.

Data controller

For the limited information the App itself handles, the controller is Ap Runner (contact details in section 17). Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through AdMob and the User Messaging Platform, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for storing and reading information on your device for advertising and for personalized advertising, including the use of your advertising ID. You give or refuse this consent in the consent message shown when you first open the App.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized or limited ads where you have not consented, and for measuring ad delivery and detecting fraud and invalid clicks. Our interest is to keep the App free of charge. You can object to this at any time by contacting us.
  • The quiz and the on-device storage of your results and settings involve no processing of personal data by us, as none of it is sent to us.

Refusing or withdrawing consent never limits any part of the quiz.

International transfers

Google may process advertising data outside your country, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses to protect such transfers.

Retention

Data stored on your device (section 6), including your consent choices, stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Advertising data collected by Google is retained according to Google’s retention policy. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to answer them.

11. Opting Out of the Advertising ID

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Reset advertising ID” (menu names may vary by device)
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

We do not provide user information to third parties, except where disclosure is required by law. The App uses the following third-party services; please refer to their policies for how they handle data:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

13. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address in section 17.

14. Your Rights

Because the App has no account and no server of ours, the only personal data we may hold about you is any email you send us, which we will delete on request. We hold nothing else that we could look up, export, or delete. You remain in control of everything the App handles:

  • Delete your results from the App’s record screen, or remove everything by uninstalling the App.
  • Where consent applies, withdraw or change your advertising consent at any time from the App’s settings screen. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 11.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection, or portability regarding data held by the advertising provider. Because that data is held by Google rather than by us, please exercise those rights through the links in section 12, or contact us at the address in section 17 and we will help where we can. We do not sell your personal information.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of your country of residence or work.

15. Security

We take appropriate measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or leakage of the information handled by the App. Your answers and results are not sent to us, and communication with the advertising services uses encrypted connections.
