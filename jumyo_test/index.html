Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Lifespan Test”

App name
Lifespan Test
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Lifespan Test” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App is a playful lifespan test: you answer seven questions about everyday habits and “what would you do?” moments and play three mini games (standing on one leg for ten seconds, a reflex test, and counting ten seconds in your head). Using your date of birth, your country and, if you choose, your sex, the App then shows a playful estimate of your lifespan, a projected last day and the time left until it, a longevity score, one of 20 result types, five sides of a long life, the answers that added the most years, short notes on what research found, and a short longevity fact. The questions change every time.

1. Summary

  • Your date of birth, sex, country, answers and results are never sent to us. Your result is worked out entirely on your device. We have no server that receives any of them.
  • There is no account registration and no login. The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or address.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your date of birth, sex, country, answers or results.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks
  • Ad measurement through the operating system’s privacy-preserving advertising features, where available (on Android, the Privacy Sandbox’s ad topics and attribution; on iOS, Apple’s SKAdNetwork)

Advertising data is never combined with your answers, results, or anything else the App stores on your device.

3. Your Profile, Answers and Results

  • Your profile. Before your first result, the App asks for your date of birth, the country or region you live in, and, if you choose, your sex (“prefer not to say” is always available). They are used only on your device to look up the life expectancy for your country, sex and age in statistical tables built into the App (United Nations, World Population Prospects 2024). You can change them at any time from the App’s settings screen.
  • Worked out on your device only. Your profile, the answers you choose (some are about everyday habits such as exercise, sleep, food, smoking or alcohol), your results in the mini games, and the results worked out from them are all computed and stored on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party, unless you choose to share a result (section 7).
  • No name or contact details. The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or address.
  • No camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location. The App does not ask for access to any of these, and does not use location services.

4. Network Use

The test works entirely offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), as described in sections 2 and 10. Before loading an ad, the App may look up the ad server’s name to check that it is online; no information about you is sent in that check.
  • Links you tap. Opening a store page or this policy hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

The advertising service starts when you open the App, so that an ad can be loaded in advance. A full-screen ad may be shown just before each result is revealed. Results are never hidden behind an ad. Banner ads appear at the bottom of the settings and FAQ screens.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To run the test and show your result
  • To keep your record of results, so you can see your past results and which types you have met
  • To work out your lifespan estimate from your profile (on your device only)
  • To avoid repeating questions you have already seen
  • To remember your settings, such as your display language
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects, using what you tell us and the aggregated statistics provided by the app stores

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored on your device, is never transmitted by the App to our servers or to any third party unless you choose to share a result (section 7), and is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App:

  • Your profile: date of birth, country or region, and sex if you gave it
  • Your record of results (your latest 100; older ones are removed automatically): for each test, the date and time, the profile it was worked out from, the answers you chose, your mini game results, your five scores, your longevity score, the estimated lifespan and projected last day, the type it revealed, and the surprising side
  • The numbers of the questions already shown (the latest 120), so the next test can avoid them
  • Your chosen display language
  • For the review prompt: how many tests you have finished, when you chose “later”, and whether it has been shown

Your advertising consent choices are also stored on your device. Google’s advertising services read them to decide how ads may be served, and they may be sent with ad requests.

Backups. On Android, the App turns off device backup and transfer, so this data is not copied to your Google account or to a new device. On iOS, it may be included in the backups you make of your device with iCloud or a computer. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, and we cannot access them.

You can delete your results one at a time, or all at once, from the App’s record screen, and change your profile from the settings screen. There is no account and no sign-in, so your records are not linked to your identity.

7. Sharing Your Result

When you tap a share button, the App draws an image of your result on your device and hands it, with a short text, to the X app or to the operating system’s share sheet. The image is written to the App’s temporary storage only for sharing and is never sent to us. Where it goes is your choice, and the app you share to applies its own privacy policy. On Android, to offer posting straight to X, the App checks on your device whether the X app is installed; this check is not sent anywhere. Sharing the App from the settings screen hands only a short text and the App’s store link to the share sheet.

8. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). When to ask is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

9. Entertainment Purpose and Data Sources

The App is for entertainment only. The lifespan, the projected last day and every other number it shows are a playful estimate made from national statistics and published research; they do not predict anyone’s life, and the App is not a medical or diagnostic tool and gives no medical advice. Please ask a doctor or another qualified professional about your health. If thinking about lifespan makes you feel heavy or anxious, please close the App and talk to someone you trust or a local helpline. Short notes on research and longevity facts shown in the App are for interest only.

Life expectancy data: United Nations, Department of Economic and Social Affairs, Population Division (2024), World Population Prospects 2024, used under CC BY 3.0 IGO and modified for the App. The United Nations does not endorse the App. Country names: Unicode CLDR. These tables are built into the App and need no network connection.
