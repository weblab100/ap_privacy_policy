Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Piano Metronome”

App name
Piano Metronome
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Piano Metronome” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App, including the advertising and the in-app purchase the App offers.

1. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration or login. If you write to us by email (Section 14, or “Contact & feedback” in the App’s settings), we receive your email address and what you write; see “Email inquiries” below.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect the following:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

Information related to purchases

When you buy Piano Metronome Pro, the payment is handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). The App receives only the result needed to unlock the purchased features, such as the product purchased, the transaction status, and the purchase date. See Section 5 for details.

Email inquiries

Owners of Piano Metronome Pro can open an email to us from the App’s settings. The App fills in only the subject and, below your message, the App’s version and your device’s platform (Android or iOS), so we can answer without asking about your setup; you can delete them before sending. The email is sent by your own mail app, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use your email address and message only to reply to you.

What the App does not access

The App only plays sounds; it does not use the microphone, camera, contacts, photos, or location services. Key presses from a keyboard or a Bluetooth page-turner pedal are used only to control the metronome on your device and are not recorded.

2. Purpose of Use

  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To provide purchased features and restore purchases
  • To respond to your inquiries

The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own. To fix defects, we only look at the aggregated crash and performance statistics that Google Play and the App Store provide to developers (on iOS, only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers).

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Your metronome settings, the pieces you save (titles, tempos, and their tempo history), and your practice time log are stored only on your device. The App also keeps on your device whether you own Piano Metronome Pro, so the purchased features work offline. This data is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party. Uninstalling the App, or using “Reset data” in the App’s settings, deletes it (Reset data keeps the purchase status; a purchase can always be restored, see Section 5). If you choose to share a piece as a code, the code (the piece’s title and metronome settings, without your tempo history or practice log) is passed to the app you pick in your device’s share sheet. When you add a piece from a code, the App reads your clipboard only at that moment, and only to read the code. The App’s fonts and sounds are bundled with it, so using them does not require any network request.

4. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

5. In-App Purchase

  • The App offers “Piano Metronome Pro”, a one-time purchase that removes ads and adds extra click sounds, detailed practice statistics, and email support. It is not a subscription and is never billed again. The price and contents are shown in the App before you buy. All other features of the App remain free without it.
  • Payment is processed by Apple or Google under your App Store or Google Play account. We never receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment details.
  • To unlock the purchased features, the App receives the purchase information provided by the store (product, transaction ID, and purchase date) and stores the result on your device. There is no server of ours involved.
  • You can restore a previous purchase on a new or reinstalled device with “Restore purchase” in the App, using the same store account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google according to their policies.

For how the stores handle your data, please refer to the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Provision to Third Parties

We do not sell user information, and we do not provide it to third parties except as described here or where disclosure is required by law. The advertising information listed in Section 1 is collected directly by Google through the AdMob SDK. Google, and the advertising partners it works with, process it as independent controllers under their own privacy policies; we do not receive it in a form that identifies you. Google may process this information outside your country, including in the United States, using safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. For how advertising data is handled, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google’s advertising partners: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/9012903
  • Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase (payments): see Section 5

7. Consent for Personalized Advertising

For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), a consent management platform certified under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework, before any advertisement is requested. If you do not consent, Google may show non-personalized or limited ads, which do not use your past activity to choose ads. You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from “Ad privacy settings” on the App’s settings screen. On iOS, any request for permission to track is made through Apple’s AppTrackingTransparency framework; all features remain available if you decline.

8. Your Rights in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland

If the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, or the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection applies to you, the following also applies.

Controller

For the App, the controller is Ap Runner (contact details in Section 14). For the advertising information collected by Google, Google is an independent controller (see Section 6).

Legal basis

  • Personalized advertising, and storing or reading identifiers on your device for advertising: your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).
  • Non-personalized or limited advertising, ad measurement, and the prevention of fraud and invalid clicks: our legitimate interest in funding a free app (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), together with your consent where the law requires consent to access your device.
  • Providing and restoring Piano Metronome Pro: performance of the contract you entered by buying it (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • Replying to your email: taking steps at your request and, for Pro owners, the support that comes with the purchase (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)), and otherwise our legitimate interest in answering you (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • Data stored only on your device (Section 3): we do not collect it, so it is not processed by us.

Retention

We do not keep any user data on our own servers. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up, and then deleted. Data stored on your device remains there until you delete it (Section 3). Google keeps advertising information for the periods described in its retention policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal data; to restrict or object to its processing; to data portability; and to withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. To withdraw or change your consent, use “Ad privacy settings” in the App. Apart from emails you send us, we hold no information that identifies you, so requests about advertising data are best made to Google (https://policies.google.com/privacy#infochoices); you may also contact us at the address in Section 14 and we will help. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

9. Opting Out of the Advertising ID

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Reset advertising ID” (menu names may vary by device)
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

10. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. In the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, consent for personalized advertising should be given by a parent or guardian for users below the age of digital consent in their country (13 to 16, depending on the country).

11. Disclaimer Regarding App Content

The App is a practice tool for musicians. Tempo terms (such as Andante or Allegro) and their tempo ranges are shown as a general guide based on the markings of traditional pendulum metronomes; sources and editions differ, and they are not a rule for any particular piece. Practice suggestions in the App are general information and do not replace the guidance of a music teacher. Please listen at a comfortable volume, especially with headphones or earphones. Composer and piece names may appear only as examples.

12. Security

We take appropriate measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or leakage of the information handled by the App.
