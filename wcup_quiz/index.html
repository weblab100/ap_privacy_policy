Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “World Cup Trivia Quiz”

App name
World Cup Trivia Quiz
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “World Cup Trivia Quiz” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App is a multiple-choice trivia quiz about the FIFA World Cup and soccer, with question sets at three difficulty levels. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App, including the advertising the App shows.

1. Summary

  • Your answers and scores never leave your device. We have no server that receives data from the App.
  • There is no account registration and no login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or address.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect advertising identifiers and device information. Ad services never receive your answers or scores.
  • The App is free and has no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner ads and full-screen (interstitial) ads. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising identifiers (the Android Advertising ID; on iOS, the IDFA only if you allow tracking as described in section 8)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

The App does not ask for access to your camera, photos, location, contacts, microphone, or any other device feature or data.

3. Your Quiz Results

The answers you choose and the score of each question set are processed only on your device. Your latest score for each set is saved on your device so that you can see your progress. Quiz results are never sent to us, to advertisers, or to any other third party. You can delete all saved results at any time with “Reset Progress” in the App’s settings screen.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device, is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • Your score for each question set and difficulty level
  • Your display language setting
  • How many times you have opened the App, to decide when to show the review prompt
  • Whether the review prompt should no longer be shown, and how many times and when you chose “Later”
  • Your advertising consent choices (stored by Google’s User Messaging Platform)

5. Network Use

All quiz questions are built into the App, so you can play offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform, as described in sections 2 and 8. Before loading a full-screen ad, the App checks whether your device is online by looking up the address of google.com through your device’s normal network settings; this check contains no information about you or your quiz results.
  • Links you tap. Opening a link such as this policy hands the address to your browser, which then applies its own privacy policy.

6. Purpose of Use

  • To run the quiz and show your score
  • To save your results so you can see your progress
  • To remember your language setting
  • To show the review prompt at a suitable time
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App, including a full-screen ad when you finish a question set
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects

7. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

8. Advertising and Consent

For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, and where otherwise required by law, the App asks for your advertising consent through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) when you first open it. Where this applies, you can change your choices at any time from “Ad Privacy Settings” in the App’s settings screen.

On iOS, Apple’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking when asked through App Tracking Transparency. If you do not allow it, ads are still shown but are not personalized with the IDFA. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Refusing or withdrawing consent never limits the quiz.

Data controller

For the limited information the App itself handles, the controller is Ap Runner (contact details in section 15). Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through AdMob and the User Messaging Platform, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for storing and reading information on your device for advertising and for personalized advertising, including the use of your advertising identifier.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized or limited ads where you have not consented, and for measuring ad delivery and detecting fraud and invalid clicks. Our interest is to keep the App free of charge. You can object to this at any time by contacting us.
  • The quiz, and on-device storage of your results and settings, involve no processing of personal data by us, as everything stays on your device.

International transfers

Google may process advertising data outside your country, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses to protect such transfers.

Retention

Data stored on your device (section 4) stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Advertising data collected by Google is retained according to Google’s retention policy. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to answer them.

9. Opting Out of Advertising Identifiers

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Reset advertising ID” (menu names may vary by device)
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, then turn off tracking for the App, or turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track” for all apps

10. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide user information to third parties, except where disclosure is required by law. The App uses the following third-party services; please refer to their policies for how they handle data:

  • Google AdMob / User Messaging Platform (advertising and consent): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

11. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16, or the age set by local law, where a higher age applies, such as in parts of the EEA), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from such children. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address in section 15.

12. Your Rights

Because the App has no account and no server of ours, we hold no personal data about you that we could look up, export, or delete on request. You remain in control of everything the App handles:

  • Delete your saved quiz results with “Reset Progress” in the App’s settings screen, or remove everything the App stores on your device by uninstalling it or clearing its data in your device settings.
  • Change your advertising consent at any time as described in section 8, and reset or delete your advertising identifier as described in section 9.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection, or portability regarding data held by the advertising provider. Because that data is held by Google rather than by us, please exercise those rights through the links in section 10, or contact us at the address in section 15 and we will help where we can. We do not sell your personal information.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of your country of residence or work.

13. Security

We take appropriate measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or leakage of the information handled by the App. Your quiz results never leave your device, and communication with the advertising services uses encrypted connections.

14. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be revised due to changes in laws or in the App’s features. The revised policy takes effect once posted on this page, and the date below is updated at the same time. Where a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will say so clearly at the top of this page and, where the law requires it, ask for your consent again, for advertising through the consent message in the App.
