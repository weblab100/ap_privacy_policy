Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Protractor Master: Angle Meter”

App name
Protractor Master: Angle Meter
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Protractor Master: Angle Meter” (the “App”). The App turns your phone into a protractor: you drag two lines on the screen, or over a photo you take or choose, and the App shows the angle between them.

1. Summary

  • Your angles and your photos are never sent to us or to anyone else. Measuring happens on your device only, and we have no server that receives data from the App.
  • The App uses the camera and your photos only when you choose to measure on a photo, and only the photo you take or pick.
  • There is no account registration and no login. The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your photos or your measurements from the App.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (determined by Google from the network connection; the App itself does not use your location)
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks
  • Ad measurement through the operating system’s privacy-preserving advertising features, where available (on Android, the Privacy Sandbox’s ad topics and attribution)

On iOS, the App does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.

Advertising data is never combined with your photos, your measurements, or anything else the App stores on your device.

3. Camera and Photos

  • Camera. The App asks for camera permission when you choose to take a photo to measure on. The photo is taken with your device’s camera screen.
  • Saving camera photos. A photo you take in the App is saved to your device’s photo library (gallery) right after it is taken, so you can measure it again later. On iOS, and on Android 9 or earlier, the App asks for permission to add photos before saving.
  • Choosing a photo. When you pick a photo from your library, the App uses the system photo picker, so it can read only the one photo you select. The App does not ask for access to your whole photo library.
  • On your device only. The photo is shown on your screen while you measure. It is never uploaded, and the App does not keep its own copy. A temporary working copy may remain in the App’s cache folder until the system clears it, you clear the App’s storage, or you uninstall the App.
  • No other access. The App does not use your location, microphone or contacts, and it uses no motion or other sensors.

You can refuse or withdraw camera and photo permissions in your device’s settings at any time; measuring on the screen keeps working without them.

4. Network Use

All measuring works without an internet connection. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), as described in sections 2 and 9. Before loading an ad, the App checks on your device whether a network connection is available; no information about you is sent in that check, and no ad is loaded while you are offline.
  • Links you tap. Opening this policy or the store hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

Banner ads appear on the settings screen and the FAQ screen. A full-screen ad may be shown from time to time when you open the settings or press Reset. The protractor screen itself never shows ads.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To show the protractor and the angles you measure, on the screen or on a photo
  • To save photos you take to your photo library
  • To remember your settings, such as display language, theme and the left or right position of the protractor
  • To decide when to show the first-time guide, a full-screen ad and a request for a store review
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects, using what you tell us and the aggregated statistics provided by the app stores

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored on your device, is never transmitted by the App to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App:

  • Your settings: display language, theme (light, dark or automatic), and whether the protractor is on the left or right
  • Whether you have seen the first-time guide
  • How many times you have opened the App, and whether and when the store review prompt has been shown or postponed
  • A count of settings and Reset actions, used only to decide when to show a full-screen ad

Your measured angles are not stored. Photos you take are saved to your photo library as described in section 3; they then belong to your library, and uninstalling the App does not delete them. Your advertising consent choices are also stored on your device. Google’s advertising services read them to decide how ads may be served, and they may be sent with ad requests.

Backups. On Android, the settings above may be included in your device’s backup to your Google account if you have backup turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Google, and we cannot access them. On iOS, they may be included in the backups you make of your device with iCloud or a computer, which are controlled by you and Apple.

You can remove all of this data at any time by clearing the App’s storage in your device’s settings or by uninstalling the App. There is no account and no sign-in, so this data is not linked to your identity.

7. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review / App Store). When to ask is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

8. Accuracy of Measurements

The App shows the angle between the lines you place. Results depend on how precisely you align the lines, and angles measured on a photo can differ from the real object because of the camera angle and lens distortion. Please do not rely on the App alone where precision matters for safety, such as construction or engineering work; check with a dedicated measuring tool.
