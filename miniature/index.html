Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Lilipo.”

App name
Lilipo.
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Lilipo.” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App makes your photos and videos look like miniature models, as a tilt-shift lens does, and can also take photos and, on Android, videos with your device’s camera while showing them live as miniatures: it keeps the part you choose sharp and blurs the rest, gives them the color you choose, can speed videos up, and then lets you save or share the result. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App, including the advertising and in-app purchases the App offers.

1. Summary

  • Your photos and videos never leave your device. Every miniature and color effect, including on photos and videos you take with the App’s camera and its live preview, runs entirely on your device. We have no server that receives your photos or videos.
  • There is no account registration and no login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or address.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your photos, videos, or edits.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your payment card or billing details.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

Advertising data is never combined with your photos, videos, edits, or anything else the App stores on your device.

Information related to purchases

When you make an in-app purchase, the payment is handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). The App receives only the result needed to unlock the purchased features, such as the product purchased, the transaction status, and the purchase date. See section 8 for details.

3. Photos and Videos

The App needs your photos and videos to do its job. How they are handled:

  • Choosing photos and videos. You choose a photo or video with the picker provided by the operating system. The App can read only the items you choose, not the rest of your library. Videos are supported on Android only.
  • Camera and microphone. When you open the App’s camera, it asks for permission to use the camera where the operating system requires this. The camera is used only while that screen is open, to show the live picture with the miniature effect you choose, to take still photos and, on Android, to record videos. When you switch the camera to video, the App asks for permission to use the microphone, and records sound only while a video is being recorded, as part of that video; if you do not allow it, videos are recorded without sound. A photo you take is given the miniature effect and the color you chose on your device, and opened in the editor; it is not added to your library until you save it. A video you record is kept in the App’s temporary storage and opened in the editor, where you can finish and save it. Photos, videos and sound are never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Applying a look to more photos. You can choose several more photos with the system picker and save each of them with the same focus, color and settings. They are processed on your device in the same way and saved to your library as new photos.
  • On-device processing. The blur, the colors, the darker edges, the speeding up of videos, and the final image or video are all computed on your device by the App itself. The App uses no face detection, no recognition of people or objects, and no other analysis of what your photos show. Photos and videos are never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Saving to your photo library. When you tap “Save”, the App saves the edited photo (JPEG) or video (MP4) to your library, first asking for permission to add items to it where the operating system requires this. It does not read, change, or delete other items in your library. On Android 9 and earlier, storage write permission is used only for this purpose.
  • Photos saved as short videos. On Android, you can save a photo as a short video (MP4) in which the focus moves. The video is made on your device from the edited photo, has no sound, and is saved or shared in the same way as other videos.
  • Sharing. When you tap “Share”, the edited photo or video is handed to the operating system’s share sheet. Where it goes is your choice, and the app you share to applies its own privacy policy.
  • Metadata. Images saved or shared by the App are made anew from their pixels and carry no metadata from the original, such as the location where it was taken, the time it was taken, or the camera model. Videos are written anew frame by frame and do not carry the location or camera details of the original. The original photo or video is never modified.
  • Sound in videos. A video saved at its own speed keeps the original video’s sound as it is; a sped-up video is saved without sound. The App does not otherwise change or remove voices or other sounds, so please check the sound before you share a video saved at its own speed.

You can revoke photo library, camera and microphone access at any time in your device settings. You can still choose photos and videos with the system picker; saving to the library, or opening the camera, will ask again.

4. Network Use

Photo and video editing works entirely offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform, as described in sections 2 and 9.
  • Purchases. Communication with the App Store or Google Play to buy, verify, and restore purchases.
  • Links you tap. Opening a store page, this policy, or an email to us hands the address to your browser, store app, or mail app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To give your photos and videos the miniature effect and the color you choose, to speed videos up, to take photos and videos with the camera while showing that effect live, and to let you save and share the results
  • To restore the photo you were editing when you return to the App
  • To remember your settings, such as the display language, the looks you saved and the look you last saved
  • To show full-screen ads only after a set number of photos or videos opened from your library
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To provide purchased features and restore purchases
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device, is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • The photo you are editing, at most 4,096 pixels on the long side so it can be saved at full quality, with a smaller copy of at most 2,000 pixels used while editing, together with your edits (where the picture stays sharp and how the rest blurs, the color and its strength, and your adjustments) and undo history, so that you can pick up where you left off
  • When you edit a video, where the video you chose is kept by the system picker on your device, the part of it you chose, and the focus, color and speed settings, so you can pick up where you left off
  • A video being saved or shared, or recorded with the App’s camera, written to the App’s temporary storage before it is added to your library, handed to the share sheet or opened in the editor
  • Your settings, such as the display language
  • The looks (focus, color and settings) kept from your earlier saves, up to a set number, and those you pinned
  • The focus, color and settings you last saved, so that a new photo or the camera starts from them
  • How many photos and videos you have opened from your library, so that full-screen ads are shown only every few of them
  • Whether the review prompt has already been shown
  • Your advertising consent choices
  • Purchase status, so that Lilipo. Pro works offline

You can delete the photo you are editing at any time from the App’s settings screen. There is no account and no sign-in, so nothing you do in the App is linked to you.

7. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

8. In-App Purchases

  • The App offers “Lilipo. Pro”, a one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks additional features. It is not a subscription and is never billed again. The price and contents are shown in the App before you buy.
  • Payment is processed by Apple or Google under your App Store or Google Play account. We never receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment details.
  • To unlock purchased features, the App receives the purchase information provided by the store (product, transaction ID, and purchase date) and stores the result on your device.
  • You can restore previous purchases on a new or reinstalled device with “Restore purchases” in the App, using the same store account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google according to their policies.

For how the stores handle your data, please refer to the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

If you do not allow it, the App still works in full and ads are shown without personalization; only the relevance of the ads changes. This never limits photo editing, saving, or sharing.
