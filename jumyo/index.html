Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Life Countdown”

App name
Life Countdown
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Life Countdown” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App estimates how much time you have left from life tables and shows it as a countdown, a grid of weeks, counts of events, and a daily habit log. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App, including the advertising and the in-app purchase the App offers.

1. Information We Collect

What you enter stays on your device

To calculate your estimate, the App asks for your date of birth, your sex (or “prefer not to say”) and the country you live in, and you may optionally answer lifestyle questions (smoking, exercise, diet, sleep, alcohol, social ties, height and weight). You may also log daily habits and add people or events to the “Times left” list (for example a name such as “Mom” and their age), and name the chapters of your life with the date each one starts. All of this is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party, and it is not shared with the advertising service. We have no server that receives it.

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration or login. If you write to us by email (Section 15, or “Contact & feedback” in the App’s settings), we receive your email address and what you write; see “Email inquiries” below.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect the following:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

The advertising service does not receive your date of birth, your answers, your habit log or anything else you enter in the App.

Information related to purchases

When you buy Life Countdown Pro, the payment is handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). The App receives only the result needed to unlock the purchased features, such as the product purchased, the transaction status, and the purchase date. See Section 6 for details.

Email inquiries

Owners of Life Countdown Pro can open an email to us from the App’s settings. The App fills in only the subject and, below your message, the App’s version and your device’s platform (Android or iOS); you can delete them before sending. The email is sent by your own mail app, and nothing is sent unless you send it. We use your email address and message only to reply to you.

What the App does not access

The App does not use the microphone, camera, contacts, photos, health data from other apps, or location services. The country is chosen by you (the App only suggests one from your device’s region setting).

2. Purpose of Use

  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To provide purchased features and restore purchases
  • To respond to your inquiries

The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own. To fix defects, we only look at the aggregated crash and performance statistics that Google Play and the App Store provide to developers (on iOS, only if you have chosen to share analytics with app developers).

3. Data Stored on Your Device

Your profile (date of birth, sex, country), your answers, your habit log, your “Times left” list, your life chapters and your settings are stored only on your device. The App also keeps on your device whether you own Life Countdown Pro, so the purchased features work offline. This data is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party. Uninstalling the App, or using “Reset data” in the App’s settings, deletes it (Reset data keeps the purchase status; a purchase can always be restored, see Section 6). The life tables, country names and fonts are bundled with the App, so calculating your estimate does not require any network request.

4. Widgets, Notifications and Sharing

  • Home-screen widgets: to show the App’s widgets (days left, hourglass, progress of today, this month, this year and your life, quote of the day, times left with someone, and life in weeks), the App saves the estimated end date, your date of birth, the quotes for the next few days, the name and count of the first person on your “Times left” list, and the widgets’ labels in a storage area on your device that only the App and its own widgets can read (an App Group on iOS). Nothing leaves your device.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, the notifications are scheduled on your device by the operating system. The daily reminder contains only the day of your life and a quote; milestone notifications contain only the milestone (for example your 10,000th day or your birthday). They are not sent through any server.
  • Sharing: if you choose to share, the App creates an image with your days left and passes it to the app you pick in your device’s share sheet. It does not include your date of birth or answers. Nothing is shared unless you choose to.

5. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt, and on how many days you opened the App, are recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

6. In-App Purchase

  • The App offers “Life Countdown Pro”, a one-time purchase that removes ads and adds three extra themes and email support. It is not a subscription and is never billed again. The price and contents are shown in the App before you buy. All other features of the App, including the estimate and the widget, remain free without it.
  • Payment is processed by Apple or Google under your App Store or Google Play account. We never receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment details.
  • To unlock the purchased features, the App receives the purchase information provided by the store (product, transaction ID, and purchase date) and stores the result on your device. There is no server of ours involved.
  • You can restore a previous purchase on a new or reinstalled device with “Restore purchase” in the App, using the same store account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google according to their policies.

For how the stores handle your data, please refer to the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Controller

For the App, the controller is Ap Runner (contact details in Section 15). For the advertising information collected by Google, Google is an independent controller (see Section 7).

Legal basis

  • Personalized advertising, and storing or reading identifiers on your device for advertising: your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)).

12. Disclaimer Regarding App Content (Not Medical Advice)

The lifespan shown in the App is a statistical estimate based on published life tables (the United Nations’ World Population Prospects life tables, and for Japan the Ministry of Health, Labour and Welfare’s abridged life tables) and on hazard ratios from published studies. It is an average for people of the same age, sex and country, adjusted by your answers; it does not predict how long any individual will live, and real lifespans vary widely. The habit values (“microlives”) are population averages for lifelong habits, after Spiegelhalter (BMJ 2012), and not the effect of a single day. Nothing in the App is medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. For questions about your health, please consult a doctor. If thinking about the length of life causes you distress, please stop using the App and talk to someone you trust or a professional.
