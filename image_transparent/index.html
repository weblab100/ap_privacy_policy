Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Opacut”

App name
Opacut
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Opacut” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App removes backgrounds from photos and videos: it finds the subject, makes the background transparent, lets you refine the result and add a background, outline, or shadow, and then save or share it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App, including the advertising and in-app purchases the App offers.

1. Summary

  • Your photos and videos never leave your device. Background removal and subject detection run entirely on your device. We have no server that receives your photos or videos.
  • There is no account registration and no login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or address.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your photos, your videos, or your edits.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your payment card or billing details.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

Advertising data is never combined with your photos, your videos, your edits, or anything else the App stores on your device.

Information related to purchases

When you make an in-app purchase, the payment is handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). The App receives only the result needed to unlock the purchased features, such as the product purchased, the transaction status, and the purchase date. See section 8 for details.

3. Photos and Videos

The App needs your photos and videos to do its job. How they are handled:

  • Choosing photos and videos. You choose a photo or video with the picker provided by the operating system. The App can read only the items you choose, not the rest of your library. The App does not use the camera or the microphone and never records photos, video, or audio itself.
  • Several photos at once. With the batch cut-out feature, you choose several photos with the system picker; each is processed on your device in the same way, one after another, and kept only until you leave that screen.
  • A picture for the background. If you choose a photo to place behind the subject of a photo or video, it is chosen with the same system picker and used only for that edit.
  • On-device processing. Subject detection, removing colors, the erase and restore brushes, outline refinement, backgrounds, outlines and shadows, and the final image or video are all computed on your device. Photos and videos are never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Videos. When you open a video, the App removes its background frame by frame on your device. While you work, the App keeps the frames and the finished video in its own temporary folder and deletes them when you leave the video screen. The original sound is kept as it is in saved MOV and MP4 videos; GIFs have no sound.
  • Subject detection. The App automatically finds the subjects of a photo or video to separate them from the background. This uses Apple’s Vision framework on iOS, and Google’s ML Kit Subject Segmentation (and, for people in videos, ML Kit Selfie Segmentation) on Android. It runs entirely on your device. On Android, the Subject Segmentation model is downloaded by Google Play services and the Selfie Segmentation model is included in the App; your photos and videos are not sent. The App does not perform face recognition and does not identify people.
  • Saving to your photo library. When you tap “Save”, the App asks for permission to add items to your library and saves the edited image (PNG) or video there (a transparent MOV on iPhone and iPad, an MP4 with a green screen or another background, or a GIF). It does not read, change, or delete other items in your library. On Android 9 and earlier, storage write permission is used only for this purpose.
  • Sharing. When you tap “Share”, the edited image or video is handed to the operating system’s share sheet. Where it goes is your choice, and the app you share to applies its own privacy policy.
  • Metadata. Images and videos saved or shared by the App do not carry over the original’s metadata, such as the location where it was taken or the camera model. The original photo or video is never modified.

You can revoke photo library access at any time in your device settings. You can still choose photos and videos with the system picker; only saving to the library will ask again.

4. Network Use

Photo and video editing works entirely offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform, as described in sections 2 and 9.
  • Purchases. Communication with the App Store or Google Play to buy, verify, and restore purchases.
  • Subject detection model (Android). Download of the Subject Segmentation model by Google Play services.
  • Links you tap. Opening a store page, this policy, or an email to us hands the address to your browser, store app, or mail app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To edit your photos and videos and let you save and share the results
  • To restore the photo you were editing when you return to the App
  • To remember your settings, such as the display language
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To provide purchased features and restore purchases
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device, is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • The photo you are editing, reduced to at most 2,000 pixels on the long side, together with your edits (subjects kept, colors removed, brush strokes, outline settings, background, outline and shadow) and undo history, so that you can pick up where you left off
  • While you edit a video, its working files (the frames and the finished video), kept in the App’s temporary folder and deleted when you leave the video screen
  • Your settings, such as the display language
  • Whether hints and the review prompt have already been shown
  • Your advertising consent choices
  • Purchase status, so that purchased features work offline

You can delete the photo you are editing at any time from the App’s settings screen. There is no account and no sign-in, so nothing you do in the App is linked to you.

7. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

8. In-App Purchases

  • Payment is processed by Apple or Google under your App Store or Google Play account. We never receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment details.
  • To unlock purchased features, the App receives the purchase information provided by the store (product, transaction ID, and purchase date) and stores the result on your device.
  • You can restore previous purchases on a new or reinstalled device with “Restore purchases” in the App, using the same store account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google according to their policies.

For how the stores handle your data, please refer to the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

プライバシーポリシー

モバイルアプリ「Opacut」

アプリ名
Opacut
開発者名
Ap Runner

Ap Runner（以下「当方」）は、Android および iOS 向けのモバイルアプリ「Opacut」（以下「本アプリ」）を提供しています。本アプリは、写真や動画の背景を消すアプリです。被写体を見つけて背景を透明にし、仕上がりの調整や、背景・縁取り・影の追加をしたうえで、保存・共有できます。本ポリシーでは、本アプリで提供する広告とアプリ内課金を含め、本アプリにおける情報の取り扱いを説明します。

1. 概要

  • 写真や動画が端末の外に出ることはありません。背景の削除と被写体の検出は、すべて端末の中で行います。当方は、写真や動画を受け取るサーバーを持っていません。
  • アカウント登録やログインはありません。氏名・メールアドレス・電話番号・住所は収集しません。
  • 広告は Google AdMob が配信し、広告 ID や端末情報を収集することがあります。広告サービスが写真・動画・編集内容を受け取ることはありません。
  • 購入の決済は Apple または Google が行います。当方がカード情報や請求先情報を受け取ることはありません。

2. 収集する情報

個人を特定できる情報

本アプリは、氏名・住所・電話番号・メールアドレスなど、個人を特定できる情報を収集しません。アカウント登録やログインはなく、本アプリからデータを受け取るサーバーも運用していません。

広告のために収集される情報

本アプリは広告の表示に Google AdMob を使用しています。AdMob は Google が運営しており、広告を配信する際に次の情報を自動的に収集することがあります。これらは Google が収集・処理するもので、当方が個人を特定できる形で受け取ることはありません。

  • 広告 ID（Android の広告 ID / AAID、Apple の IDFA）
  • 端末情報（OS のバージョン、機種、言語設定）
  • IP アドレスから推定されるおおよその位置
  • 表示やクリックなど、広告への反応

広告に関するデータが、写真・動画・編集内容など、本アプリが端末に保存する情報と結び付けられることはありません。

購入に関する情報

アプリ内課金の決済は App Store（Apple）または Google Play（Google）が行います。本アプリが受け取るのは、購入した商品・取引の状態・購入日など、購入した機能を有効にするために必要な結果だけです。詳しくは第8条をご覧ください。

3. 写真と動画

本アプリは、その機能のために写真や動画を使います。取り扱いは次のとおりです。

  • 写真・動画の選択。写真や動画は、OS が提供する選択画面で選びます。本アプリが読み取れるのは選んだものだけで、ライブラリのほかの写真や動画は読み取れません。本アプリはカメラやマイクを使わず、写真・動画・音声を自ら撮影・録音することはありません。
  • 複数の写真をまとめて処理。「まとめて切り抜き」では、OS の選択画面で複数の写真を選びます。各写真は同じ方法で1枚ずつ端末の中で処理され、その画面を離れるまでの間だけ保持されます。
  • 背景に使う写真。写真や動画の被写体の後ろに置く写真を選んだ場合も、同じ OS の選択画面で選び、その編集にだけ使います。
  • 端末内での処理。被写体の検出、色の削除、消しゴムと復元のブラシ、輪郭の調整、背景・縁取り・影、仕上がった画像や動画の生成は、すべて端末の中で計算します。写真や動画が当方や第三者にアップロードされることはありません。
  • 動画。動画を開くと、本アプリは端末の中で1コマずつ背景を消します。作業中は、コマの画像と仕上がった動画を本アプリ専用の一時フォルダに置き、動画の画面を離れると削除します。保存した MOV と MP4 には元の動画の音声がそのまま入ります。GIF に音声はありません。
  • 被写体の検出。本アプリは、背景と分けるために写真や動画の被写体を自動で見つけます。iOS では Apple の Vision フレームワーク、Android では Google の ML Kit Subject Segmentation（動画の人物には ML Kit Selfie Segmentation）を使い、すべて端末の中で処理します。Android では、Subject Segmentation のモデルを Google Play 開発者サービスがダウンロードし、Selfie Segmentation のモデルは本アプリに含まれています。いずれの場合も写真や動画は送信されません。本アプリは顔認識を行わず、人物を特定しません。
  • 写真ライブラリへの保存。「保存」をタップすると、本アプリはライブラリへの追加の許可を求め、編集した画像（PNG）または動画（iPhone・iPad では背景が透明な MOV、グリーンバックや好きな背景の MP4、または GIF）を保存します。ライブラリのほかの写真や動画を読み取ったり、変更・削除したりすることはありません。Android 9 以前では、この目的のためだけにストレージへの書き込み権限を使います。
  • 共有。「共有」をタップすると、編集した画像や動画を OS の共有画面に渡します。共有先はご自身で選ぶもので、共有先のアプリにはそのアプリのプライバシーポリシーが適用されます。
  • メタデータ。本アプリが保存・共有する画像や動画には、撮影場所やカメラの機種など、元の写真や動画のメタデータは引き継がれません。元の写真や動画が変更されることはありません。

写真ライブラリへのアクセスは、端末の設定からいつでも取り消せます。取り消した後も OS の選択画面から写真や動画を選べます。ライブラリへの保存時にだけ、あらためて許可を求めます。
