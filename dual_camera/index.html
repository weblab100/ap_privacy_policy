Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Tuvami.”

App name
Tuvami.
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Tuvami.” (the “App”). The App takes photos and records videos with your device’s front and back cameras — at the same time where the device allows it, otherwise one right after the other — and puts them together in one picture — one camera in a small window over the other, or the two stacked or side by side — then lets you change the layout, enlarge, move or mirror each camera’s picture, save the result to your photo library, or share it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App, including the advertising it shows.

1. Summary

  • Your photos, videos and sound never leave your device. Shooting, combining the two cameras, and saving all happen on your device. We have no server that receives your photos or videos.
  • There is no account registration and no login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or address.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your photos, videos or sound.
  • The App offers no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and full-screen (interstitial) advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

Advertising data is never combined with your photos, videos, or anything else the App stores on your device.

3. Camera, Microphone, Photos and Videos

  • Camera. The App asks for permission to use the camera when it opens. It uses the front and back cameras only while its camera screen is open, to show their live pictures, to take photos and to record videos. On devices that cannot run both cameras at once, it takes the back photo and then the front photo right after; for a video, it records the back camera first and then the front camera, playing the back recording on screen meanwhile.
  • Microphone. The first time you record a video, the App asks for permission to use the microphone. Sound is recorded only while a video is being recorded, as part of that video. If you do not allow it, videos are recorded without sound.
  • Saving to your photo library. The App adds a photo or video to your library only when you tap Save, and asks for permission to add to it where the operating system requires this. The App does not read your existing photo library.
  • Sharing. When you tap Share, the photo or video is handed to the share sheet of your operating system, and the app you choose there receives it under its own privacy policy.
  • On-device processing. Combining the two cameras, the layout, enlarging, moving and mirroring each camera’s picture, the edge and the date stamp are all computed on your device by the App itself; a video is combined on your device when you save or share it. The App uses no face detection, no face recognition and no cloud processing. Saved photos and videos are newly composed, so they carry no location or other shooting data.

4. Network Use

Taking, combining and saving photos and videos works entirely offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform, as described in sections 2 and 8.
  • Links you tap. Opening a store page, this policy, or another page linked from the App hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To show both cameras live, take photos and record videos with them, combine them in the layout you choose, and let you save and share the results
  • To keep your shots in the App’s album so you can change their layout later
  • To remember your settings, such as the display language and whether the selfie camera is mirrored
  • To show full-screen ads only after a set number of finished shots
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To respond to inquiries you send us by email
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device, is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • The App’s album: for each shot, the photos from the front and back cameras (for a video, each camera’s recording and, once you save or share it, the combined video), a small preview, the date it was taken, and how you laid it out, enlarged, moved or mirrored it
  • Your settings: the display language, whether the selfie camera is mirrored, and whether the front and back photos are also saved separately. The camera’s layout, frame, date stamp and self-timer are not kept; they start from their defaults each time the App opens
  • Which explanations and tips have already been shown, so they are not shown again
  • How many shots you have finished, so that full-screen ads are shown only every few of them
  • Whether the review prompt has already been shown
  • Your advertising consent choices

You can delete a shot from the album at any time, or every shot at once from the App’s settings screen. Photos and videos you saved to your photo library stay there. There is no account and no sign-in, so nothing you do in the App is linked to you.

7. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

8. Consent for Personalized Advertising

For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, and where otherwise required by law, the App requests consent for personalized advertising through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where this applies, you can review and change your consent choices at any time from the App’s settings screen.

On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalized advertising only if you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow it, the App still works in full and ads are shown without personalization; only the relevance of the ads changes. This never limits taking, saving, or sharing photos and videos.

Data controller

For the limited information the App itself handles, the controller is Ap Runner (contact details in section 15). Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through AdMob and the User Messaging Platform, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for storing and reading information on your device for advertising and for personalized advertising, including the use of your advertising ID. You give or refuse this consent in the consent message shown when you first open the App.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized or limited ads where you have not consented, and for measuring ad delivery and detecting fraud and invalid clicks. Our interest is to keep the App free of charge. You can object to this at any time by contacting us.
  • Taking and combining photos and videos, and keeping your album and settings on your device, involve no processing of personal data by us, as everything stays on your device.

Refusing or withdrawing consent never limits taking, saving, or sharing photos and videos.

International transfers

Google may process advertising data outside your country, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses to protect such transfers.

Retention

Data stored on your device (section 6), including your consent choices, stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Advertising data collected by Google is retained according to Google’s retention policy. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to answer them.

9. Opting Out of the Advertising ID

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Reset advertising ID” (menu names may vary by device)
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it. 12. Your Rights

Because the App has no account and no server of ours, we hold no personal data about you that we could look up, export, or delete on request. You remain in control of everything the App touches:

  • Delete shots from the App’s album, or every shot at once from the settings screen, or remove everything by uninstalling the App.
  • Withdraw or change your advertising consent from the App’s settings screen where it applies, and reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 9.
  • Revoke camera, microphone and photo library access at any time from your device settings.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection, or portability regarding data held by the advertising provider. Because that data is held by Google rather than by us, please exercise those rights through the links in section 10, or contact us at the address in section 15 and we will help where we can. We do not sell your personal information.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of your country of residence or work.

13. Security

We take appropriate measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or leakage of the information handled by the App. Your photos, videos and album stay on your device, and communication with the advertising services uses encrypted connections.
