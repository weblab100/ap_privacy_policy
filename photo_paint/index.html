Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Drawella”
App name
Drawella
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Drawella” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App lets you draw, write and place stickers on your photos and, on Android, on your videos, and then save or share the result, including a video of your drawing appearing stroke by stroke. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App, including the advertising and in-app purchases the App offers.

1. Summary

  • Your photos and videos never leave your device. All drawing, blurring and saving runs entirely on your device. We have no server that receives your photos, videos or drawings.
  • There is no account registration and no login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or address.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your photos, videos, or drawings.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple or Google. We never receive your payment card or billing details.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

Advertising data is never combined with your photos, videos, drawings, or anything else the App stores on your device.

Information related to purchases

When you make an in-app purchase, the payment is handled by the App Store (Apple) or Google Play (Google). The App receives only the result needed to unlock the purchased features, such as the product purchased, the transaction status, and the purchase date. See section 8 for details.

3. Photos and Videos

The App needs your photos and videos to do its job. How they are handled:

  • Choosing photos and videos. You choose a photo or video with the picker provided by the operating system. The App can read only the items you choose, not the rest of your library. Drawing on videos is available on Android only.
  • Camera. When you choose to take a photo, the App opens the camera through the operating system, which asks for permission where required. The photo you take is opened in the App to draw on; it is not added to your library until you save it. The App does not record sound and does not use the microphone.
  • On-device processing. Your drawings, text, stickers, the blur pen, and the final image or video are all computed on your device by the App itself. The App uses no face detection, no recognition of people or objects, and no other analysis of what your photos and videos show. Photos and videos are never uploaded to us or to any third party.
  • Saving to your photo library. When you tap “Save to Photos”, the App saves the result as a photo (JPEG) or video (MP4) to your library, first asking for permission to add items to it where the operating system requires this. It does not read, change, or delete other items in your library. On Android 9 and earlier, storage write permission is used only for this purpose.
  • Drawing videos. On Android, you can save a video (MP4) of your drawing appearing on a photo in the order you drew it. It is made on your device, has no sound, and is saved or shared in the same way as other videos.
  • Videos you draw on. A video you draw on is saved with its original sound, as it is. Please check the sound before you share it.
  • Sharing. When you tap “Share”, the result is handed to the operating system’s share sheet. Where it goes is your choice, and the app you share to applies its own privacy policy.
  • Metadata. Images saved or shared by the App are made anew from their pixels and carry no metadata from the original, such as the location where it was taken, the time it was taken, or the camera model. Videos are written anew frame by frame and do not carry the location or camera details of the original. The original photo or video is never modified.

You can revoke photo library and camera access at any time in your device settings. You can still choose photos and videos with the system picker; saving to the library, or taking a photo, will ask again.

4. Network Use

Drawing and saving work entirely offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform, as described in sections 2 and 9.
  • Purchases. Communication with the App Store or Google Play to buy, verify, and restore purchases.
  • Links you tap. Opening a store page, this policy, or an email to us hands the address to your browser, store app, or mail app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To let you draw, write and place stickers on your photos and videos, and to save and share the results
  • To restore the drawing you were working on when you return to the App
  • To remember your settings, such as the display language
  • To show a full-screen ad when you open a photo or video, and only every other time
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To provide purchased features and restore purchases
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device, is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • The photo you are drawing on, as a copy of at most 2,000 pixels on the long side, together with the photo as you chose it (at most 4,096 pixels) when it is larger, so it can be saved at the highest quality, and what you drew on it, so that you can pick up where you left off
  • When you draw on a video, where the video you chose is kept by the system picker on your device, and what you drew on it
  • A video being saved or shared, written to the App’s own storage before it is added to your library or handed to the share sheet, and removed the next time the App starts
  • Your settings, such as the display language
  • How many photos and videos you have opened and saved, so that full-screen ads are shown only every other time and the review prompt appears at the right time
  • Your advertising consent choices
  • Purchase status, so that Drawella Pro works offline

You can delete the drawing in progress at any time from the App’s settings screen. There is no account and no sign-in, so nothing you do in the App is linked to you.

7. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

8. In-App Purchases

  • The App offers “Drawella Pro”, a one-time purchase that removes ads and unlocks additional features. It is not a subscription and is never billed again. The price and contents are shown in the App before you buy.
  • Payment is processed by Apple or Google under your App Store or Google Play account. We never receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment details.
  • To unlock purchased features, the App receives the purchase information provided by the store (product, transaction ID, and purchase date) and stores the result on your device.
  • You can restore previous purchases on a new or reinstalled device with “Restore purchase” in the App, using the same store account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple or Google according to their policies.

For how the stores handle your data, please refer to the Apple Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Consent for Personalized Advertising

For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, and where otherwise required by law, the App requests consent for personalized advertising through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can review and change your consent choices at any time from the App’s settings screen.

On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalized advertising only if you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow it, the App still works in full and ads are shown without personalization; only the relevance of the ads changes. This never limits drawing, saving, or sharing.

Data controller

For the limited information the App itself handles, the controller is Ap Runner (contact details in section 16). Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through AdMob and the User Messaging Platform, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for storing and reading information on your device for advertising and for personalized advertising, including the use of your advertising ID. You give or refuse this consent in the consent message shown when you first open the App.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized or limited ads where you have not consented, and for measuring ad delivery and detecting fraud and invalid clicks. Our interest is to keep the App free of charge. You can object to this at any time by contacting us.
  • Drawing, and on-device storage of your drawing in progress and settings, involve no processing of personal data by us, as everything stays on your device.

Refusing or withdrawing consent never limits drawing, saving, or sharing.

International transfers

Google may process advertising data outside your country, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses to protect such transfers.

Retention

Data stored on your device (section 6), including your consent choices, stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Advertising data collected by Google is retained according to Google’s retention policy. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to answer them.

10. Opting Out of the Advertising ID

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

13. Your Rights

Because the App has no account and no server of ours, we hold no personal data about you that we could look up, export, or delete on request. You remain in control of everything the App touches:

  • Delete the drawing in progress from the App’s settings screen, or remove everything by uninstalling the App.
  • Withdraw or change your advertising consent at any time from the App’s settings screen, and reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 10.
  • Revoke photo library and camera access at any time from your device settings.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection, or portability regarding data held by the advertising or payment providers. Because that data is held by Google or Apple rather than by us, please exercise those rights through the links in sections 8 and 11, or contact us at the address in section 16 and we will help where we can. We do not sell your personal information.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of your country of residence or work.

14. Security

We take appropriate measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or leakage of the information handled by the App. Your photos, videos and drawings stay on your device, and communication with the advertising and store services uses encrypted connections.
