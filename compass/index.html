Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Simple Compass”

App name
Simple Compass
Package name
com.aprunner.compass
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Simple Compass” (the “App”). The App is an offline compass: it uses your device’s magnetic sensor to show your heading, the eight directions and the angle in degrees. If you turn on the optional GPS feature, it also shows the true north heading and your current latitude, longitude and altitude.

1. Summary

  • Your heading and your location are never sent to us or to anyone else. They are worked out and shown on your device only. We have no server that receives data from the App.
  • Location is used only if you turn on GPS in the App, and only while the App is in use. Your location is not stored.
  • There is no account registration and no login. The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your heading or location from the App.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (this is determined by Google from the network connection, not from the App’s GPS feature)
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks
  • Ad measurement through the operating system’s privacy-preserving advertising features, where available (on Android, the Privacy Sandbox’s ad topics and attribution)

On iOS, the App does not ask for permission to track you (App Tracking Transparency), so the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used.

Advertising data is never combined with your heading, your location, or anything else the App stores on your device.

3. Sensors and Location

  • Magnetic and motion sensors. The App reads your device’s magnetic sensor (and related motion sensors) to work out your heading. This happens on your device only. Sensor readings are not stored and are never sent anywhere.
  • Location (optional GPS feature). GPS is off until you turn it on in the App’s settings. When it is on, the App asks for location permission and reads your precise location only while the App is in use (never in the background). Your location is used on your device only, to calculate the magnetic declination for the true north heading and to show your latitude, longitude and altitude. Your location is not stored and is never sent to us, to advertising services, or to any other third party. You can turn GPS off in the App at any time, or withdraw location permission in your device’s settings; the compass keeps working without it.
  • No camera, microphone, photos or contacts. The App does not ask for access to any of these.

4. Network Use

The compass works entirely offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), as described in sections 2 and 9. Before loading an ad, the App checks on your device whether a network connection is available; no information about you is sent in that check, and no ad is loaded while you are offline.
  • Fonts. The first time a screen is shown, the App may download its typeface (Noto Sans JP) from Google Fonts and keep it on your device. As with any download, Google receives your IP address and basic request information. No information from the App’s features is sent.
  • Links you tap. Opening this policy or the store hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

Banner ads appear on the settings screen and the FAQ screen. A full-screen ad may be shown from time to time when you open the settings or re-measure your heading. The compass screen itself never shows ads over the compass.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To show your heading and, if you turn on GPS, the true north heading and your coordinates
  • To remember your settings, such as GPS on/off, display language and theme
  • To decide when to show a full-screen ad and when to ask for a store review
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects, using what you tell us and the aggregated statistics provided by the app stores

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored on your device, is never transmitted by the App to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App:

  • Your settings: whether GPS is on, your chosen display language, and your theme (light, dark or automatic)
  • How many times you have opened the App, and whether and when the store review prompt has been shown or postponed
  • A count of settings and re-measure actions, used only to decide when to show a full-screen ad

Your location and your heading are not stored. Your advertising consent choices are also stored on your device. Google’s advertising services read them to decide how ads may be served, and they may be sent with ad requests.

Backups. On Android, this data may be included in your device’s backup to your Google account if you have backup turned on. Those backups are controlled by you and Google, and we cannot access them. On iOS, it may be included in the backups you make of your device with iCloud or a computer, which are controlled by you and Apple.

You can remove all of this data at any time by clearing the App’s storage in your device’s settings or by uninstalling the App. There is no account and no sign-in, so this data is not linked to your identity.

7. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review). When to ask is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

8. Accuracy of Readings

The App shows readings from your device’s sensors and, if turned on, GPS. Smartphone magnetic sensors usually have an error of several degrees, and readings can be thrown off by metal objects, magnets, speakers or other electronic devices nearby. Calibrating (moving your device in a figure-8) may improve accuracy. Please do not rely on the App as your only means of navigation in situations where safety matters, such as mountaineering or at sea; carry a dedicated compass and map.
