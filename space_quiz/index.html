Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Space Trivia Quiz”

App name
Space Trivia Quiz
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Space Trivia Quiz” (the “App”). The App is a trivia quiz about space and astronomy, with an explanation for every question, available in several languages.

1. Summary

  • Your answers, scores and settings stay on your device. We have no server that receives data from the App.
  • There is no account registration and no login. The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details, and has no place to type them.
  • The App asks for no special permissions such as location, camera, microphone, photos or contacts.
  • The quizzes work without an internet connection. The App connects to the internet only for advertising and consent, and when you tap a link.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your answers or scores.
  • The App contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools, and has no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID). On iOS, the App does not ask for permission to track, so the IDFA is not available to ads
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (determined by Google from the network connection; the App itself does not use your location)
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks
  • Ad measurement through the operating system’s privacy-preserving advertising features, where available (on Android, the Privacy Sandbox’s ad topics and attribution; on iOS, SKAdNetwork)

Advertising data is never combined with your answers, scores or anything else the App stores on your device.

3. Permissions

The App uses only the internet connection and network state, which the advertising service needs. On Android, the advertising service also uses the permission to read the advertising ID. The App does not access your location, camera, microphone, photos, files or contacts.

4. Network Use

All quiz questions and explanations are built into the App, so you can play without an internet connection. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), as described in sections 2 and 8. Before loading an ad, the App checks whether it can reach the internet; when it can’t, no ad is shown.
  • Links you tap. Opening this policy, the store page of this App, or the store pages of our other apps hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy. On Android, links to our other apps include a tag that tells the store they came from this App and from which screen; it contains no information about you.

Ads appear as banners on the Settings and FAQ screens. A full-screen ad may be shown after you finish a quiz set, before your result. The quiz and explanation screens never show banner ads.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To show the quizzes and explanations in the language you choose
  • To record your best score for each quiz set
  • To decide when to show the what’s-new message, a link to our other apps, and a request for a store review
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects, using what you tell us and the aggregated statistics provided by the app stores

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored on your device, is never transmitted by the App to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App:

  • Your display language
  • Your best score for each quiz set (you can reset them from Settings)
  • How many times you have opened the App, and whether and when the store review request has been shown or postponed
  • Whether you have seen the what’s-new message, and a count of finished quizzes used to decide when to show a link to our other apps

Your advertising consent choices are also stored on your device. Google’s advertising services read them to decide how ads may be served, and they may be sent with ad requests.

Backups. Your device’s own backup (for example Android backup to your Google account, or iCloud and computer backups on iOS) may include the settings and scores above. These backups are controlled by you and by Google or Apple; we cannot access them.

7. Store Review Request

The App may ask whether you would like to rate it. Only if you tap the button to rate does the App open the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review / App Store) or the store page. When to ask is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

8. Consent for Personalized Advertising

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and where otherwise required by law, the App requests consent for personalized advertising through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) when you open it. You can review and change your consent choices at any time from “Ad privacy” in the App’s Settings, where this option appears if consent applies to you.

Data controller

For the limited information the App itself handles, the controller is Ap Runner (contact details in section 16). Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through AdMob and the User Messaging Platform, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for storing and reading information on your device for advertising and for personalized advertising, including the use of your advertising ID. You give or refuse this consent in the consent message shown when you open the App.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized or limited ads where you have not consented, and for measuring ad delivery and detecting fraud and invalid clicks. Our interest is to keep the App free of charge. You can object to this at any time by contacting us.
  • Your scores and settings stored on your device involve no processing of personal data by us, as none of it is sent to us.

Refusing or withdrawing consent never limits any quiz feature of the App.

International transfers

Google may process advertising data outside your country, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses to protect such transfers.

Retention

Data stored on your device (section 6), including your consent choices, stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Advertising data collected by Google is retained according to Google’s retention policy. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to answer them.

9. Opting Out of the Advertising ID

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Reset advertising ID” (menu names may vary by device)
  • iOS: The App does not request tracking permission. You can also turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track” in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking

10. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide user information to third parties, except where disclosure is required by law. The App uses the following third-party services; please refer to their policies for how they handle data:

  • Google AdMob / User Messaging Platform (advertising and consent): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

11. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address in section 16.

12. Your Rights

Because the App has no account and no server of ours, the only personal data we may hold about you is any email you send us, which we will delete on request. We hold nothing else that we could look up, export or delete. You remain in control of everything the App handles:

  • Reset your best scores from the App’s Settings at any time.
  • Remove all data the App stores by uninstalling it.
  • Where consent applies, withdraw or change your advertising consent at any time from “Ad privacy” in the App’s Settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 9.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection or portability regarding data held by the advertising provider. Because that data is held by Google rather than by us, please exercise those rights through the links in section 10, or contact us at the address in section 16 and we will help where we can. We do not sell your personal information.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of your country of residence or work.

13. Security

Your scores and settings are kept in the App’s private storage on your device and are not sent to us. Communication with the advertising services uses encrypted connections.

14. Links to Other Apps and Websites

The App contains links to our other apps on the store and to this policy. Once you leave the App, the destination’s own privacy policy applies.

15. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be revised due to changes in laws or in the App’s features. The revised policy takes effect once posted on this page, and the date below is updated at the same time. Where a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will notify you in the App and seek your consent again if the law requires it.
