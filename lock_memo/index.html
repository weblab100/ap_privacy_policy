Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Lokmo”

App name
Lokmo
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Lokmo” (the “App”) for Android. The App lets you write a memo, style it, place it on a background or one of your photos, and set the result as your lock screen wallpaper, so the memo stays on your lock screen without notifications. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App, including the advertising and in-app purchases the App offers.

1. Summary

  • Your memos and photos stay on your device. Memos are written, drawn, and set as your wallpaper entirely on your device. The App does not send your memos or photos to us or to anyone else, and we have no server that receives them. If you have turned on Android’s backup, Android may include them in your own Google account backup (see section 7).
  • There is no account registration and no login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or address.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your memos or your photos.
  • Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment card or billing details.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

Advertising data is never combined with your memos, your photos, or anything else the App stores on your device.

Information related to purchases

When you make an in-app purchase, the payment is handled by Google Play. The App receives only the result needed to unlock the purchased features, such as the product purchased, the transaction status, and the purchase date. See section 8 for details.

3. Your Memos and the Lock Screen

  • Writing memos. The text you write, its style (font, colors, frame, size), its position, and the state of checklist items are stored only on your device. The App does not upload them to us or to any third party.
  • Setting the lock screen wallpaper. When you tap the lock button, the App draws your memo and its background into an image on your device and sets it as your lock screen wallpaper through Android’s wallpaper service. This uses the “set wallpaper” permission, which Android grants without a prompt. The App does not change your home screen wallpaper. When you edit a memo that is on your lock screen, the App redraws the wallpaper on your device.
  • Visible to anyone who sees your phone. A memo on your lock screen can be read without unlocking your device. Please avoid putting passwords, personal numbers, or other sensitive information in a memo you place there.
  • Removing it from the lock screen. “Take off the lock screen” clears the App’s lock screen wallpaper, and your lock screen then shows the same wallpaper as your home screen. Because Android does not let apps read your previous lock screen wallpaper, the App cannot read, copy, or restore it.
  • Reading the screen size. To draw the wallpaper at the correct size, the App reads your screen size and how much your device enlarges wallpapers. This is used only for drawing and is not stored or sent.
  • Saving as an image. When you choose “Save as image”, the App saves the memo as a PNG image in the “Pictures/Lokmo” folder of your device. It does not read, change, or delete other items in your photo library. The saved image is newly drawn by the App and carries no metadata such as location.

4. Photos

You can use one of your photos as the background of a memo. How it is handled:

  • Choosing a photo. You choose a photo with the picker provided by the operating system. The App can read only the photo you choose, not the rest of your library. The App does not use the camera or the microphone.
  • Keeping a copy in the App. So that your memo keeps its background even if you later delete the original from your gallery, the App keeps a copy of the chosen photo in its own private storage, reduced to at most 2,560 pixels on each side. The App removes location metadata (where the photo was taken) from this copy. The copy is deleted when no memo uses it any longer. The App does not modify the original photo.
  • Placing a memo behind the subject (optional). If you turn on this effect, the App finds the main subject of the photo (for example a person, an animal, or an object) so that the memo can be drawn behind it. This uses Google’s ML Kit Subject Segmentation and runs entirely on your device, in a separate process of the App. The model is downloaded by Google Play services; your photo is not sent. The result (a mask of the subject’s shape) is stored next to the photo copy in the App’s private storage. The App does not perform face recognition and does not identify people.
  • ML Kit diagnostics. As described in Google’s ML Kit data disclosure, ML Kit may send Google limited diagnostic and usage information, such as the device model, OS version, the App’s package name, and performance metrics, to maintain and improve the service. According to Google, this does not include your photos, the detected subject, or your memo text.

5. Network Use

Writing memos and setting them as your lock screen wallpaper work entirely offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform, as described in sections 2 and 9.
  • Purchases. Communication with Google Play to buy, verify, and restore purchases.
  • Subject detection model and diagnostics. Download of the Subject Segmentation model by Google Play services, and the ML Kit diagnostics described in section 4.
  • Links you tap. Opening a store page or this policy, sharing the App with a friend, or writing an email to us hands the address or text to your browser, store app, the app you share to, or your mail app, which then applies its own privacy policy. An email started from the App’s Settings is prefilled with the App’s version, your device model, and your Android version, to help us answer; you can delete them before sending.

6. Purpose of Use

  • To let you write memos and show them on your lock screen
  • To keep your memos, photos, and settings when you return to the App
  • To place a memo behind the subject of a photo when you turn this on
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To provide purchased features and restore purchases
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects

7. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored on your device, is not sent by the App to us or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • Your memos: their text, style, position, background, and the state of checklist items
  • Copies of the photos you chose as backgrounds, and the subject masks described in section 4
  • Which memo is currently on your lock screen
  • Whether hints and guides have already been shown
  • The language you chose in Settings
  • Your advertising consent choices
  • Purchase status, so that purchased features work offline

The undo history is kept in memory only and is discarded when you close the App. You can delete a memo at any time from the memo list, and the photo copy it used is deleted with it. There is no account and no sign-in, so nothing you do in the App is linked to you.

If you have turned on Android’s backup, Android may include the App’s data in your device backup to your Google account, so that your memos can be restored on a new device. This backup is performed by Google under your account settings; we cannot access it. You can turn it off in your device settings.

8. In-App Purchases

  • The App may offer in-app purchases that remove ads or unlock additional features. The price, contents, and whether a purchase is one-time or recurring are shown in the App and on Google Play before you buy.
  • Payment is processed by Google Play under your Google account. We never receive or store your payment card number, billing address, or other payment details.
  • To unlock purchased features, the App receives the purchase information provided by Google Play (product, transaction ID, and purchase date) and stores the result on your device.
  • You can restore previous purchases on a new or reinstalled device with “Restore purchases” in the App, using the same Google account.
  • Refunds and the management of any subscription are handled by Google Play according to its policies.

For how Google handles your data, please refer to the Google Privacy Policy.
