Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Space Live”

App name
Space Live
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Space Live” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App is a 3D solar system viewer: it shows the Sun, the eight planets and the Moon at any date and time, eclipse shadows, the International Space Station, and satellites in orbit around the Earth. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect the following:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

2. Camera (AR Mode)

The App offers an optional AR mode that places the solar system in the space around you, so it needs access to the camera. How the camera is handled:

  • The camera image is used on your device, in real time, only as the live background behind the 3D scene, together with the surface and motion tracking provided by ARCore (Android) or ARKit (iOS).
  • No camera image, photo, video, or scan of your surroundings is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted. Each frame is drawn on screen and then discarded.
  • The camera is used only while AR mode is open. It is released when you leave AR mode or the App goes to the background.
  • The App does not perform face detection, object recognition, or any analysis of the people or places in view.
  • You can revoke camera access at any time in your device settings. AR mode will then be unavailable, but every other feature of the App keeps working.

AR mode requires ARCore-capable hardware on Android and an ARKit-capable device on iOS. It is optional; the App is fully usable without it.

3. Location

The App does not request or use your device’s location. Positions of the planets, the Moon, the ISS, and satellites are computed from the date and time alone, as seen from the centre of the Earth or from the scene camera you control. Latitude and longitude shown in the App are the ground track of the spacecraft being displayed, not yours. (As noted in section 1, advertising services may still derive an approximate location from your IP address.)

4. Network Use

The solar system, eclipse calculations, and 3D models run entirely offline on your device. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Orbital data. The App downloads publicly available orbital elements (TLE / GP data) for the ISS and for active satellites from CelesTrak. These requests ask only for public orbital data. They contain no personal information, no identifier created by the App, and no camera or location data. As with any web request, CelesTrak receives your IP address and standard connection information; please refer to the CelesTrak usage policy. The App requests this data at most once every 2 hours and 5 minutes, and falls back to data saved on your device or bundled with the App when there is no connection.
  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob, as described in section 1.
  • Links you tap. Opening a store page or an external reference link hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

The 3D scene is rendered inside a WebView that loads only files bundled with the App, served from your device over a local address (127.0.0.1) that is not reachable from outside your device. No web page from the internet is loaded into it.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To display the current and requested positions of solar system bodies and satellites
  • To remember your display settings between launches
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device, is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • Your display settings (scale, orbit lines, labels, auto-rotation, starfield density, clock format, ISS update on launch)
  • Whether the first-launch gesture hint and scale hint have been shown
  • The downloaded public orbital data and the time it was last requested, so that the App does not download it again unnecessarily

You can clear your settings at any time from “Reset settings” on the App’s settings screen. There is no account and no sign-in, so nothing you do in the App is linked to you.

7. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

11. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Disclaimer Regarding App Content

The App is an educational and entertainment astronomy viewer. Positions, distances, phases, eclipse times, and satellite tracks are calculated approximations, not navigational, observational, or operational data, and must not be relied upon for navigation, aviation, spacecraft operations, or any purpose where an error could cause harm or loss.

Planetary and lunar positions are computed with the Astronomy Engine library. Satellite and ISS positions are computed with the SGP4 model from public orbital elements published by CelesTrak, which reflect the state of the orbit at a past observation time; manoeuvres, atmospheric drag, and the age of the data all introduce error, and the App therefore shows satellite positions only within a few days of each observation time. Sizes, distances, and orbits are frequently shown at an adjusted scale so that they are visible on screen; the “real scale” mode makes distances and radii proportional, but the App is a visualisation, not a scientific instrument.

The App is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NASA, CelesTrak, Celestrak LLC, the International Space Station partner agencies, or any satellite operator or space agency. Organisation, mission, and satellite names are used solely for identification and descriptive purposes and remain the property of their respective holders. While we strive for accuracy, the App does not guarantee that its content or its data is complete, current, or free from error.
