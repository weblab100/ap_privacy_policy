Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Lunadial”

App name
Lunadial
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Lunadial” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App shows the Moon as it appears in your sky: its current phase in 3D, moonrise and moonset times, a monthly Moon calendar, upcoming full Moons, new Moons and eclipses, and a tour of the lunar surface. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements (banner ads and full-screen interstitial ads). AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

We do not combine advertising data with anything else the App holds. Your settings and the observing location you choose in the App are never sent to the advertising service.

2. Location

The App does not request or use your device’s location, and it asks for no location permission. Moonrise and moonset times and the tilt of the Moon depend on where you are, so the App works from a city instead of from your position:

  • On first launch the App picks a likely city from two settings already on your device: the region of your language setting and your time zone. No positioning (GPS, Wi-Fi, or cell) is involved.
  • You can choose a different city at any time from a list of world cities bundled with the App.
  • The chosen city is stored only on your device and is used only for calculations performed on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else.

(As noted in section 1, advertising services may still derive an approximate location from your IP address.)

3. Compass and Other Device Features

On the “Sky” screen you can tap the compass button to turn the sky diagram to the direction your phone is facing. For this the App reads the device’s orientation sensor (the rotation vector sensor on Android, or the compass heading on iOS):

  • Only the direction the phone is pointing is read. No position is obtained, and no permission is required.
  • The sensor runs only while the compass is switched on.
  • The readings are used on screen in real time only. They are never recorded, stored, or transmitted.

The App does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, photos, or files on your device, and it requests no permission for them.

4. Notifications

If you turn on “Moon alerts”, the App asks for permission to show notifications and then schedules reminders for full Moons, new Moons, and eclipses visible from your chosen city. These are local notifications: they are scheduled on your device by the App itself. We operate no push-notification server and receive no notification token. On Android, the App re-schedules the alerts after your device restarts so that they are not lost; the permission to run at startup is used only for this. You can turn the alerts off in the App or in your device settings at any time.

5. Network Use

All astronomical calculations, the 3D Moon, the calendar, the list of events, and the city list run entirely offline on your device. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob, as described in section 1.
  • Links you tap. Opening this policy, a store page, or another external link hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

The 3D scene is rendered inside a WebView that loads only files bundled with the App, served from your device over a local address (127.0.0.1) that is not reachable from outside your device. No web page from the internet is loaded into it.

6. Purpose of Use

  • To display the Moon’s phase, position, and rise and set times for the date, time, and city you select
  • To remember your settings between launches
  • To deliver the Moon alerts you have asked for
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks

7. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device, is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • Your settings (chosen city, language, place-name labels, auto-rotation, starfield density, whether Moon alerts are on)
  • Whether the first-launch hint has been shown
  • Your advertising consent choices, a count of launches and taps used to time the review prompt and the full-screen ads, and whether the review prompt has already been shown

You can reset your display and alert settings at any time from “Reset settings” on the App’s settings screen; your chosen city and language are kept. To remove everything, uninstall the App. There is no account and no sign-in, so nothing you do in the App is linked to you.

8. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

9. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide user information to third parties, except where disclosure is required by law. For how advertising data is handled, please refer to:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

10. Consent for Personalized Advertising

For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, the App requests consent for personalized advertising through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where such consent applies, you can review and change your choices at any time from the App’s settings screen.

On iOS, the App may ask for permission to track through the AppTrackingTransparency framework before the advertising identifier (IDFA) can be used for personalized advertising. If you decline, the App still works in full and ads are shown without personalization; only the relevance of the ads changes. Declining never disables any feature of the App.

11. Opting Out of the Advertising ID

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Reset advertising ID” (menu names may vary by device)
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

12. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address in section 17.

13. Disclaimer Regarding App Content

The App is an educational and entertainment astronomy viewer. Phases, positions, distances, rise and set times, and eclipse times are calculated approximations, not navigational, observational, or operational data, and must not be relied upon for navigation, tide prediction, religious observance that depends on an official sighting, or any purpose where an error could cause harm or loss.

Rise and set times are computed for the centre of the city you choose against a sea-level horizon; terrain, buildings, and atmospheric conditions change what you actually see. Lunar relief is exaggerated for legibility, earthshine and the colour of a lunar eclipse are drawn brighter than in reality, and the background stars are decorative rather than the real sky. Full-Moon names, zodiac signs, and the term “supermoon” are cultural or popular conventions, not scientific classifications, and the App makes no astrological, medical, or agricultural claim. Never look at the Sun without certified eclipse protection, including during a solar eclipse listed in the App.

The App is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NASA, the IAU, the USGS, GeoNames, or any space agency or mission named in it. Organisation, mission, and place names are used solely for identification and descriptive purposes and remain the property of their respective holders. While we strive for accuracy, the App does not guarantee that its content is complete, current, or free from error.

Attribution for third-party material used in the App, including lunar imagery from Solar System Scope, terrain data from NASA, place names from the IAU / USGS Gazetteer of Planetary Nomenclature, city data from GeoNames, and the open-source libraries, is shown inside the App on the settings screen.

Because that data is held by Google rather than by us, please exercise those rights through the Google links in section 9, or contact us at the address in section 17 and we will help where we can.
