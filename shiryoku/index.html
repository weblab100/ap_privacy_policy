Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Eye Test & Training”

App name
Eye Test & Training
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Eye Test & Training” (the “App”) for Android. The App lets you check the sharpness of your vision, eye by eye, in a playful way: you answer which way the gap of a ring (a Landolt ring) faces by swiping, at reading distance or about 2 metres away with a helper or a mirror. It also has an astigmatism check, a red-green focus check, short eye exercises, seeing games, and a daily fact about eyes. The App is free with ads; an optional one-time purchase, “Eye Test & Training Pro”, removes the ads and adds more (section 5).

1. Summary

  • Your results are never sent to us. Every result is worked out and kept on your device. We have no server that receives your answers or results.
  • There is no account registration and no login. The App never asks for or collects your name, age, date of birth, gender, email address, phone number, or address.
  • The App does not use the camera, the microphone, your photos, your contacts, or your location.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your answers or results.
  • The optional one-time purchase (Pro) is handled by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. With Pro, no ads are shown and the advertising service is not started.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply.

Information collected for advertising

Without Pro, the App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks
  • Ad measurement through the operating system’s privacy-preserving advertising features, where available (the Android Privacy Sandbox’s ad topics and attribution)

Advertising data is never combined with your answers, results, or anything else the App stores on your device.

3. Your Answers and Results

  • Worked out on your device only. Your swipes, the lines you pick on the astigmatism chart, your game scores, and the results worked out from them are all computed on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party, unless you choose to share a result (section 7).
  • Sizing the screen. To draw the rings at the right size, the App asks you to hold a standard-size card (such as a bank or transit card) against the screen and adjust an outline to match it. Only the resulting screen size (a number) is kept on your device. The card is never photographed or scanned, and nothing printed on it is read.
  • No personal details are needed. The App never asks for your name, age, date of birth, or gender.
  • No camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location. The App does not ask for access to any of these, and does not use location services. Vibration is used only as a cue during the test and exercises.

4. Network Use

The tests, checks and exercises work entirely offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), as described in sections 2 and 10. Before loading an ad, the App may look up the ad server’s name to check that it is online; no information about you is sent in that check.
  • Purchases. When you open the Pro offer, buy Pro or restore a purchase, the App asks Google Play for the price and for the purchases your Google account owns, and checks this again when the App starts, so a refunded purchase is noticed. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under your Google account; the App only learns whether Pro is owned. We do not operate a server that receives purchase information.
  • Links you tap. Opening a store page or this policy hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

Without Pro, the advertising service starts when you open the App, so that an ad can be loaded in advance. A full-screen ad may be shown just before a result appears (the vision test, the checks, the games and the eye exercises), while the App briefly shows that it is adding up your result. No full-screen ad is shown when you close a result. Banner ads appear at the bottom of the settings and FAQ screens. With Pro, no ads are shown, and neither the advertising service nor its consent message is started.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To run the vision test, the checks, the games and the exercises, and to show your results
  • To keep your records, so you can see past results, a chart of your far vision over time, your best game scores, and a calendar of the days you exercised
  • To provide Pro: no ads, all the kinds of 3D picture (without Pro, the black and white dots), records kept with no time limit (without Pro, the last 31 days), charts that analyse your records (your vision over all time, the gap between your eyes, your exercises by month and by weekday, and your game scores play by play), and email support
  • To remember your settings, such as your screen size, how acuity is written, and your display language
  • To choose the daily fact about eyes
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects, using what you tell us and the aggregated statistics provided by the app stores

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored on your device, is never transmitted by the App to our servers or to any third party unless you choose to share a result (section 7), and is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App:

  • Your vision test records (without Pro, those from the last 31 days; older ones are deleted automatically. With Pro, all of them, with no time limit): the date and time, how you tested (near, with a helper, or with a mirror), the result for each eye, whether you wore glasses or contact lenses, and whether the screen had been sized with a card
  • Your astigmatism check records (the date and time, and which lines looked darker to each eye; kept for the same period as the vision test records)
  • Your best scores in the games (the color game and the three ring games), and each game’s score with its date and time, for the charts (kept for the same period as the vision test records)
  • Whether Pro has been bought, as last confirmed by Google Play, so the App works offline
  • The days on which you did each eye exercise, for the streak and the calendar (kept for the same period as the vision test records)
  • How many times you finished each eye exercise on each day (kept for the same period as the vision test records)
  • The screen size from the card, how acuity is written, and your chosen display language
  • How many results you have seen in total, and, for the review prompt, when you chose “later” and whether it has been shown

Your advertising consent choices are also stored on your device. Google’s advertising services read them to decide how ads may be served, and they may be sent with ad requests.

Backups. The App turns off device backup and transfer, so this data is not copied to your Google account or to a new device. A Pro purchase belongs to your Google account and can be restored on another device with “Restore purchase”; your records cannot.

You can delete your vision test records one at a time from the App’s record screen, or delete all records at once from the settings screen. There is no account and no sign-in, so your records are not linked to your identity.

7. Sharing Your Result

When you tap “Share result”, the App hands a short text with your result and the App’s store link to the operating system’s share sheet. Nothing is sent to us. Where it goes is your choice, and the app you share to applies its own privacy policy. Sharing the App from the settings screen hands only a short text and the App’s store link to the share sheet.

8. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using Google Play In-App Review. When to ask is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

9. Not a Medical Device

The App is not a medical device. It does not detect, diagnose, treat or prevent any disease or condition, and its results are estimates, about one line of an eye chart either way: they depend on the distance to your eyes, the lighting and your screen. It does not replace an eye examination by an eye doctor, optometrist or optician; please have your eyes examined by a professional for a driving licence, school or work, or new glasses. The eye exercises are for resting and moving your eyes; they are not a treatment and do not promise to improve your vision. If your sight changes suddenly, things look distorted, you see flashes of light or many floaters, part of your view is missing, or your eye is painful or red, please see an eye doctor right away. Facts about eyes shown in the App are for interest only.
