Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Career Calling Quiz”

App name
Career Calling Quiz
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Career Calling Quiz” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App is a career quiz: you answer personality questions and play mini games, and it suggests your calling from the jobs of the country or region you chose. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App, including the advertising the App shows.

1. Summary

  • Your answers and results never leave your device. Scoring and results are all worked out on your device. We have no server that receives your answers or results.
  • There is no account registration and no login. We never ask for or collect your name, age, gender, email address, phone number, or address.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your answers or results.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

Advertising data is never combined with your answers, results, or anything else the App stores on your device.

3. Your Answers and Results

  • Worked out on your device only. What you choose in the personality questions, your mini game scores, and the balance of six types and calling worked out from them are all computed and stored on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Country or region. The country or region you choose at first is used only to show that place’s jobs and estimated salaries, and is stored on your device. The App does not use location services.
  • No camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location. The App does not ask for access to any of these.

4. Network Use

The quiz works entirely offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), as described in sections 2 and 9. Before loading an ad, the App may look up the ad server’s name to check that it is online; nothing is sent in that check.
  • Links you tap. Opening a store page or this policy hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

The advertising service is not started at all until you have seen the result of your first quiz.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To run the quiz and show your result
  • To let you look back at earlier results
  • To avoid repeating questions you have already seen
  • To remember your settings, such as your country or region and display language
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device, is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • Your quiz history (the date and time, country or region, scores for the six types, what you chose in the personality questions, the moments quoted as reasons for your result, and your calling)
  • The numbers of the questions already shown (the latest 120), so the next quiz can avoid them
  • Your chosen country or region and display language
  • Whether the review prompt has been shown
  • Your advertising consent choices

You can delete your quiz history one result at a time, or all at once, from the App’s history screen. There is no account and no sign-in, so nothing you do in the App is linked to you.

7. Sharing Your Result

When you tap a share button, the App draws an image of your result on your device and hands it, with a short text, to the X app or to the operating system’s share sheet. The image is written to the App’s temporary storage only for sharing and is never sent to us. Where it goes is your choice, and the app you share to applies its own privacy policy.

8. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

9. Consent for Personalized Advertising

For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, and where otherwise required by law, the App requests consent for personalized advertising through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can review and change your consent choices at any time from the App’s settings screen.

On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalized advertising only if you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow it, the App still works in full and ads are shown without personalization.

Data controller

For the limited information the App itself handles, the controller is Ap Runner (contact details in section 16). Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through AdMob and the User Messaging Platform, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) for storing and reading information on your device for advertising and for personalized advertising, including the use of your advertising ID. You give or refuse this consent in the consent message shown after you have seen the result of your first quiz.
  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized or limited ads where you have not consented, and for measuring ad delivery and detecting fraud and invalid clicks. Our interest is to keep the App free of charge. You can object to this at any time by contacting us.
  • The quiz and the on-device storage of your history and settings involve no processing of personal data by us, as everything stays on your device.

Refusing or withdrawing consent never limits any part of the quiz.

International transfers

Google may process advertising data outside your country, including in the United States. Google relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework (and its UK and Swiss extensions) and on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses to protect such transfers.

Retention

Data stored on your device (section 6), including your consent choices, stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Advertising data collected by Google is retained according to Google’s retention policy. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to answer them.

10. Opting Out of the Advertising ID

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → “Delete advertising ID” or “Reset advertising ID” (menu names may vary by device)
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

11. Provision to Third Parties

We do not provide user information to third parties, except where disclosure is required by law. The App uses the following third-party services; please refer to their policies for how they handle data:

  • Google AdMob / User Messaging Platform (advertising and consent): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

12. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address in section 16.

13. Your Rights

Because the App has no account and no server of ours, we hold no personal data about you that we could look up, export, or delete on request. You remain in control of everything the App handles:

  • Delete your quiz history from the App’s history screen, or remove everything by uninstalling the App.
  • Withdraw or change your advertising consent at any time from the App’s settings screen, and reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 10.

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, objection, or portability regarding data held by the advertising provider. Because that data is held by Google rather than by us, please exercise those rights through the links in section 11, or contact us at the address in section 16 and we will help where we can. We do not sell your personal information.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of your country of residence or work.

14. Security

We take appropriate measures to prevent unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or leakage of the information handled by the App. Your answers and results stay on your device, and communication with the advertising services uses encrypted connections.

15. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be revised due to changes in laws or in the App’s features. The revised policy takes effect once posted on this page, and the date below is updated at the same time. Where a change materially affects how your information is handled, we will notify you in the App and seek your consent again if the law requires it.
