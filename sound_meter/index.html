Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Sound & Vibration Meter” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration or login.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect the following:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

2. Microphone

The App measures the sound level around you, so it needs access to the microphone. How the audio is handled:

  • Audio is analyzed on your device, in real time, only to compute sound levels in decibels.
  • No audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted. The App keeps only the resulting numbers, never the sound itself.
  • The microphone is used only while the measurement screen is open. It is released when the App goes to the background.
  • You can revoke microphone access at any time in your device settings. The App will then stop measuring sound, but vibration measurement and every other screen keep working.

Motion sensor (accelerometer)

To measure vibration, the App reads the device’s built-in accelerometer. No permission is required for this sensor on iOS or Android. How the readings are handled:

  • Accelerometer readings are analyzed on your device, in real time, only to compute a vibration level. They are then discarded.
  • No raw motion data is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted. The App keeps only the resulting numbers.
  • The sensor is read only while the measurement screen is open in a vibration mode. It is released when the App goes to the background.

This data is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party. There is no account and no sign-in. Uninstalling the App deletes all of it.

10. Disclaimer Regarding Measurements

The App turns a smartphone microphone into an approximate sound level meter. It is not a certified measuring instrument and does not conform to IEC 61672 or any equivalent standard.

Smartphone microphones are designed for speech. Their sensitivity varies between models, and most devices cannot reproduce very quiet sounds or levels above roughly 90 dB correctly. Readings therefore depend on your device, its orientation, the distance to the source, wind, temperature, and any case covering the microphone.

Readings from the App are a guide, not legal or official evidence, and must not be used on their own to demonstrate compliance with occupational health rules, environmental noise regulations, tenancy agreements, or any other legal requirement. For that purpose, use a calibrated sound level meter or engage a qualified measurement professional.

The hearing safety figures shown in the App are based on published guidance from the U.S. National Institute for Occupational Safety and Health (NIOSH, 85 dBA over 8 hours with a 3 dB exchange rate) and the Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA, 90 dBA over 8 hours with a 5 dB exchange rate). They are general information and are not medical advice. If you are concerned about your hearing, consult a physician or an audiologist.

The App is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by NIOSH, OSHA, any standards body, or any manufacturer of measuring instruments. Organisation names are referred to only as statements of fact. The developer accepts no liability for any loss arising from actions taken on the basis of the App's readings.
