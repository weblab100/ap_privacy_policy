Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Velubo.”

App name
Velubo.
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Velubo.” (the “App”) for Android and iOS. The App opens a photo or a video you choose from your photo library, or a photo you take with your camera, finds its subject (a person, a pet, food or a thing) on your device, and blurs only the background, as a camera lens with a wide aperture does. You can choose how strongly the background is blurred and the look of the blur, correct the subject of a photo with your finger, then save the result to your photo library or share it. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled in the App, including the advertising it shows.

1. Summary

  • Your photos and videos never leave your device. Finding the subject, blurring and saving all happen on your device. We have no server that receives your photos or videos.
  • There is no account registration and no login. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or address.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your photos or videos.
  • The App offers no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number, or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display banner and full-screen (interstitial) advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID, or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

Advertising data is never combined with your photos, videos, or anything else the App stores on your device.

3. Camera, Photos, Videos and Finding the Subject

  • Choosing a photo or video. When you tap “Choose a Photo or Video”, the App opens the photo picker of your operating system. The App receives only the one photo or video you pick; it does not read or browse the rest of your photo library.
  • Videos. A video is read frame by frame on your device; the subject is found and the background blurred in each frame, and a new video is written on your device with the sound of the original copied as it is. The App does not record sound and does not use the microphone.
  • Camera. When you tap “Take a Photo”, the App opens your device’s camera to take one photo to blur, and asks for camera permission where the operating system requires it. The App uses the camera only then.
  • Finding the subject on your device. To tell the subject from the background, the App uses image segmentation built into your device: Apple’s Vision framework on iOS, and Google ML Kit (Subject Segmentation provided through Google Play services, or Selfie Segmentation bundled with the App) on Android. It runs entirely on your device; your photos and videos are not uploaded. It outlines the subject only; the App uses no face recognition and does not identify anyone.
  • Saving to your photo library. The App adds a photo or video to your library only when you tap Save, and asks for permission to add to it where the operating system requires this. Your original photo or video is never changed.
  • Sharing. When you tap Share, the photo or video is handed to the share sheet of your operating system, and the app you choose there receives it under its own privacy policy.
  • No location or shooting data. Saved photos and videos are newly made by the App, so they carry no location or other shooting data from the original.

4. Network Use

Choosing, blurring and saving photos and videos works offline. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform, as described in sections 2 and 8.
  • The subject model on Android. On Android, the model that finds the subject is installed by Google Play services, which may download it from Google the first time it is needed. Google ML Kit may also contact Google to receive model updates and send Google anonymous metrics about how the feature performs (such as device type and processing time). Your photos, videos and the results are never sent.
  • Links you tap. Opening a store page, this policy, or another page linked from the App hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

5. Purpose of Use

  • To find the subject of the photo or video you choose, blur its background as you set, and let you save and share the result
  • To remember your settings, such as the display language and the aperture and lens look you chose last
  • To show full-screen ads only every few photos you open
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To respond to inquiries you send us by email
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects

6. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored only on your device, is never transmitted to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted when you uninstall the App:

  • Your settings: the display language, and the aperture, lens look and strength of the balls of light you chose last
  • How many times tips have been shown, so they are not shown again and again
  • How many photos and videos you have opened, so that full-screen ads are shown only every few of them
  • Whether the review prompt has already been shown
  • Your advertising consent choices

The App keeps no album of its own. While you save or share, the finished photo or video is written to the App’s temporary cache, which the operating system clears. Photos and videos you saved to your photo library stay there. There is no account and no sign-in, so nothing you do in the App is linked to you.

7. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review or Apple SKStoreReviewController). Whether you have been shown the prompt is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

8. Consent for Personalized Advertising

For users in the EEA, the UK, and Switzerland, and where otherwise required by law, the App requests consent for personalized advertising through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where this applies, you can review and change your consent choices at any time from the App’s settings screen.

On iOS, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for personalized advertising only if you allow tracking in Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you do not allow it, the App still works in full and ads are shown without personalization; only the relevance of the ads changes. This never limits blurring, saving, or sharing photos and videos.

Refusing or withdrawing consent never limits blurring, saving, or sharing photos and videos.

Retention
