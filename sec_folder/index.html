Privacy Policy

For the mobile app “Secret Folder: Photo Vault”

App name
Secret Folder: Photo Vault
Developer name
Ap Runner

Ap Runner (“we”) provides the mobile application “Secret Folder: Photo Vault” (the “App”). The App looks and works like a calculator. When you enter your 4-digit PIN and press “=”, it opens a secret folder where you can keep photos, videos, voice memos and other files, encrypted on your device.

1. Summary

  • The photos, videos and files you put in the App are never sent to us or to anyone else. They are encrypted and stored only on your device. We have no server that receives data from the App.
  • The App reads your photo library, camera, microphone and files only when you choose to add something, and only the items you select.
  • Fingerprint and face unlock are handled by your device’s operating system. The App never receives your biometric data.
  • There is no account registration and no login. The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number or any other contact details.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may collect the advertising ID and device information. Ad services never receive your files, your PIN or anything inside the secret folder.
  • The App has no in-app purchases.

2. Information We Collect

Personally identifiable information

The App does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, address, phone number or email address. The App has no account registration and no login, and we operate no server that receives data from the App. If you email us, we receive your email address and what you write, and use them only to reply.

Information collected for advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob is operated by Google and, when it serves an ad, it may automatically collect the following. This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us; we never receive it in a form that identifies you.

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID / AAID; on iOS, the IDFA only if you have allowed tracking)
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language settings)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (determined by Google from the network connection; the App itself does not use your location)
  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks
  • Ad measurement through the operating system’s privacy-preserving advertising features, where available (on Android, the Privacy Sandbox’s ad topics and attribution)

Advertising data is never combined with your files, your PIN, or anything else the App stores on your device.

3. Your Files and Encryption

  • Encryption. Every file you add, and its thumbnail, is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored in the App’s private storage area on your device. The files can’t be opened outside the App.
  • Encryption key and PIN. The encryption key is generated on your device and, together with your PIN, is kept in the operating system’s secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). They are never sent anywhere. We can’t see, recover or reset your PIN.
  • Temporary copies. To play a video, share a file or save it to your photo library, the App briefly creates a decrypted copy in its temporary folder and deletes it right after use.
  • Exporting and sharing. When you export a file to your photo library or a folder, or share it with another app, that copy is no longer encrypted and is handled by the destination you chose.
  • Uninstalling. Uninstalling the App deletes all files inside it. Please export anything you want to keep before uninstalling.

4. Permissions

The App asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can refuse or withdraw any of them in your device’s settings; the rest of the App keeps working.

  • Photos and videos. Requested when you choose “Add from Photos”. Only the items you pick are read and copied into the secret folder. After importing, the App can delete the originals from your photo library, but only if you confirm. The App also asks for permission to add items when you export to your photo library.
  • Files. When you add from or export to your files, you choose the file or folder through the system file picker.
  • Camera. Requested when you take a photo or video in the App. What you capture is saved straight into the secret folder.
  • Microphone. Requested when you record a voice memo or a video with sound.
  • Fingerprint / Face ID. Used only if you turn on biometric unlock. Your device checks your fingerprint or face and tells the App only whether it succeeded.
  • Internet. Used only for advertising and consent, as described in section 5.

The App does not access your location or contacts.

5. Network Use

The secret folder works without an internet connection. The App connects to the internet only for the following:

  • Advertising. Communication performed by Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP), as described in sections 2 and 10.
  • Links you tap. Opening this policy or the store hands the address to your browser or store app, which then applies its own privacy policy.

Ads appear inside the secret folder (banner ads, and an ad shown among your folders) and on the FAQ screen. A full-screen ad may be shown from time to time after you add files. The calculator screen and the PIN screen never show ads.

6. Purpose of Use

  • To encrypt, store and show the files you put in the secret folder
  • To lock and unlock the secret folder with your PIN or, if you turn it on, biometrics
  • To remember your settings, such as display language, theme and biometric unlock
  • To decide when to show a full-screen ad and when to ask for a store review
  • To deliver and display advertisements within the App
  • To measure ad performance and detect invalid clicks
  • To respond to your inquiries
  • To improve the quality of the App and fix defects, using what you tell us and the aggregated statistics provided by the app stores

7. Data Stored on Your Device

The following is stored on your device, is never transmitted by the App to our servers or to any third party, and is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App:

  • Your encrypted files and thumbnails, and the folders you create
  • Your PIN and the encryption key, in the operating system’s secure storage
  • Your settings: display language, theme, whether biometric unlock is on, and whether you have accepted the terms of use and seen the guide
  • How many times you have opened the App, and whether and when the store review prompt has been shown or postponed
  • Counts of calculator views and file imports, used only to decide when to show a full-screen ad

Your advertising consent choices are also stored on your device. Google’s advertising services read them to decide how ads may be served, and they may be sent with ad requests.

Backups. On Android, the App is excluded from the device backup to your Google account, so the secret folder is never copied to the cloud by the App or by Android backup. On iOS, files may be included in backups you make of your device with iCloud or a computer, which are controlled by you and Apple; the files remain encrypted, and we cannot access them.

8. Store Review Prompt

The App may ask you to rate it on the store, using the review feature provided by the operating system (Google Play In-App Review / App Store). When to ask is recorded only on your device; we do not receive your rating or review text in any form that identifies you.

9. Please Keep Your PIN Safe

Because your PIN and encryption key never leave your device, we have no way to reset your PIN or recover your files. If you turn on biometric unlock, anyone whose fingerprint or face is registered on your device can open the secret folder. Please back up important files by exporting them regularly.

For users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, and where otherwise required by law, the App requests consent for personalized advertising through Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) when you first open it. You can review and change your consent choices at any time from “Privacy Options” in the secret folder’s menu, where this option appears if consent applies to you.

Data controller

For the limited information the App itself handles, the controller is Ap Runner (contact details in section 17). Google acts as an independent controller for the data it collects through AdMob and the User Messaging Platform, under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

  • Legitimate interests (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) for showing non-personalized or limited ads where you have not consented, and for measuring ad delivery and detecting fraud and invalid clicks. Our interest is to keep the App free of charge. You can object to this at any time by contacting us.

Refusing or withdrawing consent never limits any feature of the secret folder.

International transfers

Retention

Data stored on your device (section 7), including your consent choices, stays there until you delete it or uninstall the App. Advertising data collected by Google is retained according to Google’s retention policy. We keep emails you send us only as long as needed to answer them.

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and turn off “Allow Apps to Request to Track”

14. Your Rights

Because the App has no account and no server of ours, the only personal data we may hold about you is any email you send us, which we will delete on request. We hold nothing else that we could look up, export or delete. You remain in control of everything the App handles:

  • Export or delete any file in the secret folder at any time.
  • Withdraw photo, camera, microphone or biometric permission in your device’s settings at any time.

  • Where consent applies, withdraw or change your advertising consent at any time from “Privacy Options” in the App’s menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID as described in section 11.

Because that data is held by Google rather than by us, please exercise those rights through the links in section 12, or contact us at the address in section 17 and we will help where we can. We do not sell your personal information.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection supervisory authority of your country of residence or work.

15. Security

Files in the secret folder are encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and the key is kept in the operating system’s secure storage. Your files are not sent to us, and communication with the advertising services uses encrypted connections. No method of protection is perfect: anyone who knows your PIN, or whose biometrics are registered on your device if you turn on biometric unlock, can open the secret folder.
